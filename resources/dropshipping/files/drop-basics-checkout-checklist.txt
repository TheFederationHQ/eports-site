DROPSHIPPING - THE CHECKOUT CHECKLIST  (Chapter 5)

Build the store: one product page. Then run these five checks yourself,
on your own phone. If any answer is no, fix it before you send a single
visitor - traffic wasted on a leaking page is the most expensive kind.

  [ ] 1. The TOTAL cost including shipping is visible before checkout.
  [ ] 2. A buyer can check out WITHOUT creating an account (guest
         checkout on - it is often off by default).
  [ ] 3. The real DELIVERY WINDOW (from your sample, in days) is on the
         product page.
  [ ] 4. There is a working CONTACT method and a RETURNS policy - and
         you will honor the policy you state.
  [ ] 5. The whole thing loads and reads properly on a PHONE.

  Checked on (date): __________   Phone used: ______________________
  Fixed before launch: _______________________________________________

Two settings on that page lose more sales than any words you write:
surprise shipping costs at checkout, and forced account creation.
