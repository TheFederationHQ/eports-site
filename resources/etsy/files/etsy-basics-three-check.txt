ETSY - THE THREE-CHECK PLEDGE  (Chapter 1)

The one enemy on Etsy is platform blindness - building on rented ground
without learning the rules. It shows up as three failures: Etsy won't SHOW
you, won't PERMIT you, or won't LEAVE you enough. This pledge turns all three
into a habit you run before you ever publish or pay for a listing.

  Before I publish or pay for a listing, I check:
    1. Can Etsy FIND it?      (built to be shown)
    2. Will Etsy PERMIT it?   (inside the rules)
    3. What will Etsy LEAVE me?  (the real take-home)
