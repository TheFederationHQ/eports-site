IF IT'S STILL SITTING ON THE REVIEW DATE — FOUR STEPS, IN ORDER
=====================================================================
[ ] 1. RE-CHECK YOUR COMPS. Pull the sold listings again. Has the market
       moved? Were you optimistic?
[ ] 2. IMPROVE THE LISTING BEFORE YOU TOUCH THE PRICE. Sharper lead photo,
       clearer title, more item-specifics.
[ ] 3. ONLY THEN LOWER THE PRICE — but never below your net floor while
       you're still giving it a fair chance. Floor: $______
[ ] 4. SET A HARD EXIT DEADLINE (from fresh comps — a few weeks is
       typical): ________
       At the deadline, switch modes: stop pricing for profit, start
       getting your cash back — sell it cheap, lot it with other slow
       items, or donate it and log the zero-dollar exit.

Nothing sits forever. A miss with a routine is a managed event.
