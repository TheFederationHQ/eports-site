THE OFFER BUILDER
From "The One-Person AI Business — Basics", Chapter 5
================================================================

An offer is not a description of your product. It is a promise of a changed
situation, in the BUYER'S own words. You already ran a rough test in chapter 4,
so you are shaping from evidence — even if that evidence is only silence. Now
sharpen it until the right person thinks "that is me".

Narrowing feels like giving up customers. It is the opposite: a narrow offer is
the only kind one person can make a stranger feel SEEN by, and being seen is
what opens the wallet.


THE FOUR PARTS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  FOR ____________________________________________________________
      (the one narrow person — "small Shopify owners who lose an afternoon a
       week to platform changes", not "busy people")

  WHO STRUGGLES WITH _____________________________________________
      (the recurring annoyance, in THEIR words — the ones you collected)

  THIS GIVES THEM _______________________________________________
      (the changed situation, NOT the features — not "a 12-tab spreadsheet"
       but "your month's numbers, answered in ten minutes not an afternoon")

  UNLIKE ________________________________________________________
      (the thing they do now — a generic tool, a manual grind, nothing — and
       why yours fits where that one chafes)


NOW WRITE IT AS ONE SENTENCE THE BUYER WOULD SAY BACK TO YOU
----------------------------------------------------------------
  ______________________________________________________________
  ______________________________________________________________

Read it aloud. If the target buyer would NOD, it is right. If they would need
it explained, it is still about you, not them.


TWO WORKED EXAMPLES
----------------------------------------------------------------
  For freelance designers who lose Fridays to admin,
  who never know which projects actually made money,
  this gives them their week's numbers answered in ten minutes,
  unlike the spreadsheet they keep meaning to build and never do.

  For indie Shopify owners under 1,000 orders a month,
  who can't keep up with platform, fee and app changes,
  this gives them the one thing that changed this week and what to do,
  unlike scrolling five newsletters hoping something matters.


THE MACHINE-DRAFTS-HUMAN-DECIDES MOVE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Ask your WRITING engine: "Here is my buyer and rough offer [paste]. Write the
offer as one sentence in the buyer's own language, five ways — plainest first,
boldest last. No hype, no 'unlock', no 'revolutionary'." Then YOU pick the one
that sounds like a true thing a real person would say, and delete the rest.

⚠️ Narrow the BUYER, not the value. Making the person specific is the move.
Making the promise smaller is just undercharging your own work.
