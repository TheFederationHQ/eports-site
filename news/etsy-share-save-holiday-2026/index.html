Etsy's Share & Save pays 6.5% back until January 4. Here's when Offsite Ads cancel it

Until January 4, 2027, Etsy's Share & Save program pays 6.5% back on eligible orders that come through links you share, up from the usual 4%. That cancels out the 6.5% transaction fee on those sales. But if the buyer's last click was an Offsite Ad, you lose the refund and pay an ad fee on top. Here's how it works, and how to make sure you get it.

The short version: Until January 4, 2027, Etsy will pay you back 6.5% on eligible orders that come through links you share yourself, so a $45 order earns $2.93 back. That matches Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee, so on those sales the fee is cancelled out. The catch: if the buyer's last click was one of Etsy's Offsite Ads, you get no refund and pay an ad fee on top of the transaction fee.

The click that cancels it

The refund only counts if your link is the buyer's last click and they buy within 30 days. Both the click and the purchase have to fall inside the promotion window. If the buyer's last click was one of Etsy's Offsite Ads, the order doesn't qualify.

That's the expensive case. You lose the $2.93 refund, still pay the $2.93 transaction fee, and owe the Offsite Ads fee on top. On the $45 order, that's $6.75 in ad fees.

The ad fee is 15% of the order, or 12% once your shop has reached $10,000 in sales in any 365-day period.

Etsy's help page lists two more traps. Links sent through Etsy Messages don't count. Neither do links placed in your listing descriptions. The refund is for links you share outside Etsy, such as on social media, in emails or on your own website.

What Etsy is offering

Share & Save normally pays 4% back on orders that come through your own shop links. Etsy's holiday terms raise that to 6.5%.

The higher rate runs from 9am Eastern on October 20 to 9am Eastern on January 4, 2027. After that, it goes back to the usual rate.

Etsy's help page says the refund is worked out the same way as the transaction fee: on the order total including shipping and gift wrap, but not tax. It shows up in your payment account once the order is complete.

Payment processing and listing fees still apply. This only covers the transaction fee.

What it's worth

Take a $40 item with $5 shipping. The transaction fee is 6.5% of $45, which is $2.93.

  • At the usual 4%, Share & Save pays back $1.80.
  • Until January 4, it pays back $2.93, the whole fee.

Across 50 holiday orders like that through your links, that's $146.50 back, against $90 at the usual rate.

Who can get it

Etsy's help page says every enrolled seller qualifies, and Value Added Resource reports that sellers already in Share & Save get the higher rate automatically. Etsy's formal terms are stricter on paper: they say the promotion is by invitation. Others can join for free in Shop Manager.

You have to stay enrolled. Etsy's terms say a seller who leaves the program won't receive the holiday refund, so stay in until January 4.

Shop links need to use the yourshopname.etsy.com format. For listings and promo codes, use the trackable links Etsy makes for you in Share & Save. Etsy's help page says shop links, listing links and promo code links all count, and the Etsy Seller app can also make links for reviews and shop sections.

What to do this week

  1. Join or check: in Shop Manager, go to Marketing, then Share & Save, and press Join now if you're not in.
  2. Copy the links Etsy makes there and swap them into your social bios, emails and website.
  3. Share listing links in your posts as well as your shop link, so buyers land right on the item.
  4. Run the numbers on your best sellers with our free Etsy fee calculator.
  5. Check your first refund: after an order from your link completes, look for the Share & Save badge on the order and the refund line in your payment account. Then put January 4, 9am Eastern, in your calendar.

What's still unknown: Etsy's holiday terms say promotion invitations come from Etsy. If you don't see the higher rate in Shop Manager, contact Etsy support before the busiest weeks.
