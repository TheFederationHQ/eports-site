Kept current The platform fees the books use

Fees change more often than a book can. These are the US figures the books' worked examples rest on — checked by hand and dated below.

  • Shopify — Basic $39/month ($29 billed yearly), Grow $105 ($89), Advanced $399 ($359). Shopify Payments online cards: 2.9% + 30¢ on Basic, 2.7% + 30¢ on Grow, 2.5% + 30¢ on Advanced. Using another payment provider adds a fee: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced.
  • Stripe (your own store) — 2.9% + 30¢ per domestic card payment.
  • PayPal — PayPal Checkout 3.49% + $0.49; standard card payments 2.99% + $0.49; +1.50% on international payments.
  • Etsy (a marketplace that passes processing through) — 6.5% transaction on item + shipping, 3% + $0.25 processing, $0.20 listing.
  • TikTok Shop (US) — 6% referral fee in the published table (8% widely reported since August 2026, not confirmed in the table); a reported 3% for 30 days if your first sale comes within 60 days of onboarding.
  • Import duty — the US $800 duty-free exemption is suspended for every country, so every order your supplier ships from abroad is now dutiable (the $800 de minimis exemption is suspended), and how much depends on the product, its origin and any exceptions. Rates change often and depend on the product and where it's made: always cost from the landed price to a US address, in writing.

Checked at source October 13, 2026 (Shopify plans October 19, 2026); next review due Jan 2027. Dated copy, not a live feed — verify on each platform's own pricing page before you rely on a figure. Nothing here is legal or tax advice. The same table as a file: drop-basics-fees-current.txt.
