1. THE HOURS RULE (ch1)
=====================================================================
My ceiling: ___ hours a week.
"If a target calculates above ___ hours a week, I do not add listing or
sourcing hours. I first change a buy, a channel, a price, a process — or
a delegation gate — and rerun the calculation until it fits."

2. PROFESSIONAL-REFERRAL TRIGGERS (ch4) — any one true = book it
=====================================================================
[ ] I've made direct sales (flea market, local, pop-up) and don't know
    whether I need a seller's permit.
[ ] More than 1 sale in 20 in my records has no cost I can show.
[ ] I've had a notice or letter from any tax authority.
[ ] I'm about to pay anyone to help me.
[ ] I'm thinking about forming a business entity, or I've been told I should.
[ ] A platform has sent me a tax form I don't understand.
Tax/records -> an enrolled agent or CPA who works with small sellers.
Paying people / forming a business -> whoever handles that where you live.
Bring: the gap list, off-platform records, the platform reports.

3. THE EXCEPTIONS ROUTINE (ch5) — record at the touch
=====================================================================
INTAKE ....... every item gets a row and a cost before the shelf; box
               lots split by comp share; row code on the bag.
OFF-PLATFORM . price + row-code sticker -> sales book; cash banked by
               the next business day, reconciled to the book.
RETURNS ...... a log line the day it lands: item, reason, refund,
               return postage, outcome.
RUNNING COSTS  one mileage line per route; receipts photographed.

GATES:
[ ] No item on the shelf without a cost and a row code.
[ ] No listing live without condition grade, tested-and-measured line,
    and (where triggered) a recall check.
[ ] No next sourcing/selling session until the weekly review is done.

WEEKLY EXCEPTIONS REVIEW (four questions — a NO to all four is normal):
1. Anything in this week without a cost on its row?
2. Any off-platform sale not in the sales book, or cash not banked?
3. Any return, refund or claim not in the log?
4. This week's miles and receipts captured?

4. RETURNS & CONDITION POLICY — template (ch5)
=====================================================================
Condition scale (use the same words every time):
  Excellent — clean, complete, works as it should.
  Good ...... light wear; every mark photographed and named.
  Fair ...... works, but marked/worn/cosmetic flaw; see every photo.
  For parts or not working — doesn't fully work or is incomplete;
              say exactly what's wrong and what was tested.
Tested-and-measured line in every listing: what I checked + measurements.
Return setting: chosen from MY OWN returns count (eBay's guarantee covers
  not-as-described items whatever the setting; the seller pays return
  postage on those). Mine: _______________________________
Never sell: a recalled item, unless its recall notice / the CPSC says it
  may be resold after the remedy (check cpsc.gov/recalls for anything
  electrical, for children, or an unfamiliar brand-and-model); anything I
  can't confidently say is genuine.

7. THE OPERATING CALENDAR (ch10)
=====================================================================
Each automatic action:
  IF this number crosses this line -> THIS happens -> UNTIL this.
WEEKLY (Sunday, ~30 min): log line; exceptions review; actual beside
  forecast; age bands.
  AUTO: review not done -> no sourcing/selling session until it is.
        open-to-buy under the cash gate -> next Saturday is a clearing
        session, until it's back over the gate.
        hours over the ceiling 2 weeks running -> the lowest net-per-hour
        row is suspended for 4 weeks.
MONTHLY (first Sunday, ~1 h): reconcile account to records; net per hour
  by row; over-90 share; demand check; roll the forecast 4 weeks.
  AUTO: a row under its floor rate 2 months running -> stop-doing list,
        switched off by a date, until a quarterly re-test clears it.
        over-90 share rising 2 months running -> Saturday buying capped
        at half the per-trip limit until the share falls.
QUARTERLY (~2-3 h): re-run the time study; re-check hurdle, card, gates
  against the quarter's evidence; decide trials; check the triggers.
  AUTO: every passing item cleared the hurdle by $3+ -> hurdle up $1, dated.
        a trial at its end date -> decided by its own bands that day.
        any referral trigger true -> appointment booked that day.
YEARLY (one weekend): close the year's records with the professional;
  keep records (often three years, longer in some cases); recalculate
  the ceiling (hours x 60 / minutes per sale x net per item); write the
  choice.
  AUTO: a target above the hours ceiling -> no added hours; cut the
        target to the ceiling, or write one dated economic change.
Lines move ONLY at the quarterly review (hurdle, card, gates) or the
yearly review (ceiling, target, shape of the business). Never in an aisle.
