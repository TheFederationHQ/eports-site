A 25% off Etsy sale can cut your profit nearly in half. Here's the Black Friday maths

On a $40 item that costs you $16 to make, Etsy's fees leave you $19.75 at full price. At 25% off, you keep $10.70. That means you need about 85% more orders just to make the same money. Here's the sum, and how to choose a discount that still pays.

The short version: On a $40 Etsy item, a 25% off sale can drop what you keep from $19.75 to $10.70. Your costs stay the same while the price falls, so profit falls by almost half, and you'd need about 85% more orders to stand still. Before you set a Black Friday discount, work out what each sale will leave you. It takes about two minutes.

The sum at full price

Take a $40 item with free shipping. Say it costs you $16 all in: materials, packaging, postage and your time. If your $16 leaves out postage or labour, the amount left below is before those costs, not profit.

Our example assumes a US seller paid in dollars, one item per order, no gift wrap and no sales tax. Etsy's fee pages set three charges for that seller:

  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the price the buyer pays after your discount, so $2.60.
  • Payment processing: 3% plus $0.25, so $1.45.
  • Listing fee: $0.20, charged again when a listing with more stock sells and renews.

That's $4.25 in fees. You keep $19.75.

The same item at 25% off

At 25% off, the price is $30. The fees shrink a little, to $3.30, because two of them are percentages. But your $16 making cost doesn't shrink at all.

You keep $10.70. The price fell by a quarter. Your profit fell by 46%.

The part most sellers miss

To make the same money at 25% off, you need about 85% more orders. If you usually sell 20 of this item in Black Friday week, you'd need about 37 at the sale price to match.

Ads make it tighter. If a buyer clicks one of Etsy's Offsite Ads and orders within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% of the order, capped at $100. Shops that pass $10,000 in sales over 365 days pay 12% instead.

Below that level you can opt out of Offsite Ads in your shop settings. Above it, the ads are compulsory.

On the $30 sale, the 15% rate takes another $4.50, and you'd keep $6.20.

These figures leave out sales tax. The processing fee is charged on the whole order, including tax and any shipping you charge, and the transaction fee covers charged shipping too. With free shipping, as here, only tax adds to the fees.

The 25% question

Etsy's own Cyber Specials event runs November 23 to December 1. To be considered for Etsy's marketing, a shop had to opt in by October 27 with at least 25% off. That deadline has passed.

So 25% is the number many sellers copy, but it only bought a chance at Etsy's promotion. A shop that missed the opt-in can still run its own sale at any discount it chooses. On low-margin items, a smaller discount may keep more money than a bigger one.

What to do this week

  1. Run your own numbers for your three best sellers in our free Etsy fee calculator. Include postage and your time in the cost.
  2. Compare four prices for each: no discount, 10%, 15% and 25%. Note what each sale leaves you.
  3. Work out the orders you'd need: divide your full-price profit by your sale-price profit, then multiply by your usual orders.
  4. Count your Offsite Ads sales from last year's holiday season, so you know how many may carry the extra fee.

What's still unknown: how many extra orders a sale brings in. That depends on your shop, and last year's Black Friday numbers are your best guide.
