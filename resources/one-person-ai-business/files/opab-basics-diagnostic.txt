THE FOUR-SUSPECTS DIAGNOSTIC
From "The One-Person AI Business — Basics", Chapter 8
================================================================

A launch went quiet. Two roads: "the offer is bad, I'm not cut out for this"
(delete it, month lost) — or "which ONE thing do I change?" (change it, put it
back out, watch). Road two treats silence as information, not a verdict. This
is road two.

⚠️ Silence does not PROVE which of the four failed — one quiet launch is thin
evidence. It gives you the LIKELIEST suspect and the next test. That is a
working hypothesis, and it is enough.


WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED  (numbers, not feelings)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Roughly how many people saw it:  ______
  How many responded / engaged:    ______
  How many put money down:         ______


THE FOUR SUSPECTS  (take one at a time)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  OFFER    — is the promised change something this buyer URGENTLY wants, or
             something you find interesting?
             Signal: people say "neat" and don't act.                    [ ]

  PRICE    — check this LAST. "Lower the price" is the panic move your price
             floor armored you against.
             Signal: genuine interest that stalls exactly at payment.     [ ]

  AUDIENCE — are you in front of the RIGHT people, or merely people? A perfect
             offer in the wrong room is silence.
             Signal: no one engages at all — wrong room.                  [ ]

  ASK      — was there a clear, single, obvious next step to BUY, or did you
             describe and hope?
             Signal: warmth and replies, but no path from "yes" to "paid". [ ]


ASK YOUR REASONING ENGINE  (to think clearly when you're too close)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  "Here is my offer, my price, where I posted it, and what happened: [numbers].
   Which of offer, price, audience, or ask is MOST LIKELY the problem, and what
   is the single smallest change that would test that?"


THE RULE THAT KEEPS THE SIGNAL CLEAN
----------------------------------------------------------------
  CHANGE ONE THING, THEN MEASURE — before changing another. Change everything
  at once and, when something finally works, you won't know WHAT worked, and
  you can't repeat what you can't identify.

  My one change:  ______________________________________________
  I will put it back out by:  ______ (date)


THE OUTCOME  (write it into your operating brain, dated)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] First completed sale, $____, on ______, delivered to ____________
  [ ] No completed sale; likeliest cause: ______; evidence: ______;
      next test: ______

Both are honest results. Only "I gave up before a completed sale or a named
next test" is not — and you are not going to write that one.
