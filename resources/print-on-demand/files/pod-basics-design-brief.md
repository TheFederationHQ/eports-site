# ePORTS — Print on Demand: your design brief

I will make a ____________ for people who ____________
that says / shows ____________________________________

Check before you commit:
- [ ] a specific buyer, not "everyone"
- [ ] a group that spends on belonging
- [ ] similar things already sell (I saw recent reviews)
- [ ] cleared both safety checks (mine, no one else's)

Circle ONE idea and stop — comparing more niches is usually procrastination.
