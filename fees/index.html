Worked examples

What one sale really costs

The fees above, worked out on one sale each. US sellers, no sales tax and no ads, before the cost of the item and postage. Want your own numbers? Use our free calculators.

  • A $20 item with $5 shipping on Etsy

    Transaction fee, 6.5% of $25$1.63
    Payment processing, 3% + $0.25$1.00
    Listing fee$0.20
    Fees in total$2.83
    You keep, before your costs$22.17 of $25.00

    That’s 11.3% of the sale. Offsite Ads, if a sale comes from one, adds 15% (12% for bigger shops), capped at $100.

  • A $50 sale, shipping included, on eBay

    Final value fee, 13.6%$6.80
    Per-order fee (over $10)$0.40
    Fees in total$7.20
    You keep, before your costs$42.80 of $50.00

    That’s 14.4% of the sale.

  • A $50 item, buyer pays no shipping, on Mercari

    Selling fee, 10%$5.00
    Fees in total$5.00
    You keep, before your costs$45.00 of $50.00

    That’s 10.0% of the sale.

  • A $20 digital download through your own Gumroad link

    Gumroad fee, 10% + $0.50$2.50
    Card processing, 2.9% + $0.30$0.88
    Fees in total$3.38
    You keep, before your costs$16.62 of $20.00

    That’s 16.9% of the sale.

  • A $30 order on Shopify Basic

    Card payment, 2.9% + 30¢$1.17
    Fees in total$1.17
    You keep, before your costs$28.83 of $30.00

    That’s 3.9% of the sale. The $39 monthly plan ($29 billed yearly) comes on top.
