INSTALL THE OPERATING CADENCE — the changed world (chapter 12)

One brilliant reallocation decays. A decent one on a RHYTHM compounds
and self-corrects. Book the dates NOW — a cadence in your intentions
is a wish with good posture.

QUARTERLY (the deep one, ~half a day): the whole loop
  1 Rebuild the loaded ledger for your top designs
  2 Refresh the league table (total + per-hour)
  3 RE-ASK: which constraint binds NOW? (it MOVES — margin today,
    capacity tomorrow; a signal is a prompt to re-diagnose, not the
    answer)
  4 DRAFT the reallocation (fund/keep/fix/cut/retire)
  5 Pre-mortem the draft; patch the weak leg
  6 COMMIT; write the 1-page record; set the next date

MONTHLY (the light one, ~30 min):
  - CAC vs ceiling — breached? -> run the DEEP review now
  - any design's total moved > ~25% vs last month?
  - do THIS month's dated durability chore

BOOK IT:
  QUARTERLY deep review — first date: __________ (recurring)
    who's in it (you, later + your helper): __________________
  MONTHLY light check — first date: __________ (recurring)
  Checklist lives at (so I run it, not reinvent it): __________
  TRIGGER that pulls the deep review EARLY (a winner saturates,
    CAC breaks ceiling, a provider/platform shock): ____________

The businesses that keep compounding are the ones still running the
loop in the quiet quarters, when nothing is on fire. Keep it small
enough that you will still be doing it in two years.
