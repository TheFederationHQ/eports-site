One folder, five parts. A forecast earns its keep by making the miss
readable — and forcing a decision.

=====================================================================
THE FIVE PARTS
=====================================================================
[ ] 1. NET-PER-HOUR REPORT — the time study re-run for the four weeks,
       row by row (platform · channel · kind of item), overhead separate.
       Cash basis: $____/h   On finished work: $____/h   Judge again on: ____
[ ] 2. RECORDS AND SALES-TAX SETUP — the one-account rule · a cost on every
       row · the exceptions log · the sales-tax and resale-certificate record
       with the professional's answers and dates.
[ ] 3. RETURNS-AND-CONDITION POLICY — as published, with any changes and why.
[ ] 4. FORECAST TESTED AGAINST ACTUALS — eight-week forecast, four real weeks
       beside it (cash, hours AND the aging of new stock), the misses read,
       the changes they caused.
[ ] 5. DECISION PAGE — every expansion considered: the gates, the answer,
       and at least one growth option you decided NOT to take.
