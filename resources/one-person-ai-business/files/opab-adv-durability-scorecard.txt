THE DURABILITY SCORECARD  —  how much of this do you actually own?
From "The One-Person AI Business — Advanced", Chapter 9
================================================================

A profitable, growing business is not the same as a STRONG one. Profitability
is whether you make money today; durability is whether you survive a shock you
don't control tomorrow — a deplatforming, a frozen processor, an algorithm
change, a big customer leaving. The more leveraged you get, the more
concentrated you tend to become, so score this BEFORE it finds you. Set the
bands first; grade against them; fix worst-first.


THE BANDS  (define before you look)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  GREEN = within threshold. AMBER = a breach that hurts a quarter (or one you're
  already mitigating). RED = a breach that could END the business fast (a true
  single point of failure, or a dependency most of your revenue/acquisition rides on).


SCORE EACH AXIS   (g / a / r below = green / amber / red)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  CONCENTRATION
    Largest customer's revenue share   g<15  a15-25  r>25    → ____%  [ ]
    Largest product's revenue share    g<60  a60-75  r>75    → ____%  [ ]
    Biggest channel's share of NEW customers g<40 a40-55 r>55    → ____%  [ ]
  OPERATIONAL DEPENDENCE  (list every platform/account/processor/tool/domain that
    would sink you if it vanished tomorrow; each needs a <30-day fallback)
    g = all have a fallback · a = slow/partial · r = a single point, no fallback
    Single points of failure with NO fallback: ______________  [ ]
  OWNED-DEMAND SHARE
    New customers via a channel YOU own  g>50  a35-50  r<35   → ____%  [ ]


WHAT YOU ACTUALLY OWN
----------------------------------------------------------------
Most of what feels like an asset is RENTED (reach, platform, payment rail).
Usually only two things are genuinely yours: the OPERATING BRAIN (judgment,
archive, rules — your IP) and the AUDIENCE LIST (portable, though not immune —
deliverability, consent, domain). Build on those; use them to cut dependence on
everything else.


PRIORITY & REMEDIATION  (reds first; failure that would happen fastest or cause the greatest damage first)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Channel concentration → shift acquisition to the owned list (the flywheel).
  Operational single point of failure → a SECOND provider set up and tested (a
    <30-day fallback), plus a monthly export of your list held where YOU control it.
  Product concentration → a second real revenue line (productized judgment).
