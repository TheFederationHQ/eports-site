Kept current Etsy's fees, checked by hand

The figures the book uses for the reader's arithmetic — the fastest-changing thing in this business — checked by hand and dated below.

⚠️ The 6.5% is not the whole fee. Processing is charged on top, the listing fee is a fixed amount per sale, and Offsite Ads adds 12–15% only on the sales Etsy's own ads brought in. Always work it out at your numbers — the spreadsheet does it for you.

Etsy seller fees — per sale, unless noted

  • Transaction — 6.5% of the order: item + shipping + gift wrap (not sales tax).
  • Payment processing — charged separately: 3% + $0.25 in the US, on the total the buyer pays including shipping and sales tax. Other countries have their own rates.
  • Listing — $0.20 per listing, charged again every four months or when it sells.
  • Regulatory operating fee — none for US sellers. Outside the US: sellers in some countries pay a small one; check your own Etsy fee page.
  • Offsite Ads — 15% of the order for shops under $10,000/yr, 12% at or above it, capped at $100 per order — only on orders Etsy's ads drove; automatic, opt-out only while under $10k, then mandatory for the life of the shop.
  • Optional — a possible one-time set-up fee for some new shops (the amount is shown during setup and can vary by location); Etsy Plus at $10/month (rarely worth it for a beginner).

Checked at source October 12, 2026; next review due Jan 2027. Dated copy, not a live feed — verify at Etsy's own fees & payments policy and help pages before you rely on a figure. Nothing here is legal or tax advice.
