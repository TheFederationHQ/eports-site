THE GOVERNED-AUTONOMY TABLE  —  authority caps + a kill threshold
From "The One-Person AI Business — Advanced", Chapter 7
================================================================

To lift the ATTENTION ceiling, more must run without waiting on your judgment —
safely. Not by trusting more; by SPECIFYING once what may run alone, up to what
bound, and what trips it all back. This is the DECISION (the spec), not the
build — you write what "safe to run alone" means and hand it to whoever (or
whatever) implements it. You do NOT have to build the alarm to govern it.


STEP 1 · TIER YOUR ACTION CLASSES
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Tier 1 — zero-impact (internal drafts, suggestions)         → full autonomy
  Tier 2 — reversible, low-reach (routine content/replies)    → autonomy, capped, watched
  Tier 3 — customer-affecting (anything a customer sees/feels) → narrow, capped, closely watched
  Tier 4 — money, legal, public factual claims, binding       → NEVER autonomous

  HUMAN approves AND executes every actual movement of money. AI never approves money.


STEP 3 · THE KILL THRESHOLD  (quantitative, not a vibe)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Trips when: [correction rate > __% in a __ window] / [any red-flag event of
  severity __] / [a core metric falls below __].
  ⚠️ It BOUNDS EXPOSURE; it does NOT make autonomy "safe." It can't catch a
  correlated failure, an untraceable harm, damage under the line, or a lagging
  metric. Grant authority because the trip CAPS how bad an undetected-in-time
  failure gets — not because it can't happen.


STEP 4 · THE REVERSION PROTOCOL  (decide it before you need it)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. HALT — all Tier 2–3 autonomy drops to gated; nothing further goes out.
  2. ALERT — you're notified, with the trigger and window that fired.
  3. ASSESS — find the blast radius: what went out, who saw it, who acted.
  4. CORRECT & LEARN — fix it; turn the failure into a sharper rule/cap.
  5. RESTART — one class at a time, on evidence. Never all at once.


THE FORCING COMMITMENT  (dated)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  "From [date], ________ runs autonomously under cap: ________. Always escalates:
   ________. Money stays human-executed. Kill trigger: if ________ crosses ____,
   all Tier 2–3 autonomy reverts to gated and alerts me; I re-enable class by
   class on evidence. This bounds exposure; it does not eliminate it. I set the caps and
   answer the trip; I no longer sit at every junction."
