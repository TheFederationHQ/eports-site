RESELLING — BASICS · YOUR OPERATING COMMITMENT (chapter 1)
Free from ePORTS — eports.global/resources/reselling
Four numbers, written before you buy a single thing. Honest, not ambitious.

=====================================================================
1. YOUR HOURS
=====================================================================
Hours a week I can really give this, in a normal week (not my best week):
_____ hours
(Five is plenty to start. Eight is a lot. Write the real one.)

=====================================================================
2. YOUR CASH-AT-RISK CEILING
=====================================================================
The most money I'll have tied up in things bought but not yet sold — money
I could lose without it hurting my rent or my sleep:
$_____
Rule: when my unsold pile holds this much cash, I stop buying until
something sells.

=====================================================================
3. YOUR MINIMUM NET — tied to time
=====================================================================
The least profit, after every cost, an item must clear to be worth my hands:
$_____ for no more than about _____ minutes of hands-on work.
(A good starting rule: $15 of profit for no more than about thirty minutes.)
Below this floor I don't buy, however cheap the thing is.

=====================================================================
4. YOUR QUIT TRIGGER — a real condition, not a feeling
=====================================================================
"After _____ days, _____ items I've properly researched and listed, and
the weekly hours I committed to above — if my total profit is still under
$_____, I'll stop and reassess."
(Example: ninety days, thirty items, under $100.)
