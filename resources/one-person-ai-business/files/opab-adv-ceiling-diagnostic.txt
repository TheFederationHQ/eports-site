THE CEILING DIAGNOSTIC  —  which wall is actually holding you down
From "The One-Person AI Business — Advanced", Chapter 1
================================================================

Your business plateaued. The instinct is "do more" — but past a point, more
volume just trades back the hours you freed. Growth is jammed against a
STRUCTURAL ceiling, and each ceiling is lifted by a different move. Pour effort
into the wrong one and you lose a year. This finds the right one — from
evidence, not a feeling.

⚠️ First rule out the others honestly: if you're NOT profitable, or have no
real demand, or can't do the work — fix that first. This is for a business that
already works and has stopped growing.


THE THREE CEILINGS  (for a profitable, efficient, plateaued business)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOURS       — revenue only rises when your time rises. Best months = busiest.
  ATTENTION   — work waits on YOUR judgment; there are only so many good calls
                in you per week. Throughput capped by a decision backlog.
  POSITIONING — to a stranger you're interchangeable; can't charge more, and new
                customers only come when you personally push.


STEP 1 · THE THOUGHT EXPERIMENT  (get a candidate)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Imagine you DOUBLED each, changing nothing else. Which one, doubled, actually
moves revenue?
  [ ] Double my available hours → revenue clearly follows?        → HOURS
  [ ] Make every judgment call instantly and perfectly → throughput jumps? → ATTENTION
  [ ] A stranger instantly gets why I'm the specific choice for them → unlocks growth? → POSITIONING


STEP 2 · CONFIRM AGAINST EVIDENCE  (not a hunch)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOURS fingerprint — do revenue and your hours rise/fall together on your
    tracker?                                              yes / no  ______
  ATTENTION fingerprint — is throughput capped by a backlog of decisions only
    you can make (drafts awaiting you, calls pending)?    yes / no  ______
  POSITIONING fingerprint — run a SMALL price test on new sign-ups: did a modest
    rise cost you conversions?                                yes / no  ______
    Ask 5 recent customers why they chose you over the nearest alternative.
    Can they name something SPECIFIC only you offer? (a referral alone isn't a
    "no" — listen for the reason behind it)               yes / no  ______

  ⚠️ An untested price fear is NOT evidence — it's a hypothesis. Run the test.


STEP 3 · INTERACTING CEILINGS  (find the UPSTREAM one)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Often one binds hardest and a second sits behind it. Start with the one that,
once lifted, LOOSENS the others. (Positioning often sits upstream of hours: a
clear position makes customers cheaper to win, so the hours ceiling eases on its
own.) Ask: which one, left unfixed, makes the others impossible to fix?
