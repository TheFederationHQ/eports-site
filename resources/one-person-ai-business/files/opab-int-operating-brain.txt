PART 4 · THE DECISION RULES  (the actual product of this chapter)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  The things you decide without thinking, written as if-then rules AI can apply.
  The test of a good rule: it removes a judgement call from your day. "Be
  helpful" removes nothing. Write at least TEN, drawn from your chapter-1 log —
  for the function you're about to hand off, every judgement call, as a rule.

  [ ] ____________________________________________________________


ASK YOUR WRITING ENGINE  (the test — and the loop that IS the skill)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Give it the whole brain and one real piece of the function's work. Read what
  comes back. Every place it went wrong is a MISSING RULE — add it, run again.
  When it gets a piece right BECAUSE a rule told it how, the brain is working.

  ⚠️ You won't get every rule right, and you don't need to. The brain is a
  LIVING document: every call you disagree with, all book long, the fix is not
  to scold the AI — it's to add or sharpen the rule that would have prevented
  it. That habit, more than any single rule, is what you're building.
