Kept current Platform fees, checked by hand

The figures the books use for the reader's arithmetic — the fastest-changing thing in this business — checked by hand and dated below.

⚠️ A percentage is not the whole fee. Every platform below also charges a fixed amount per sale, so the effective rate is higher on a cheap product than on an expensive one. Always work it out at your price — the pricing calculator does this for you.

Selling platforms — platform cut + payment processing, per direct sale

  • Whop — no platform fee on a plain download since early 2026, just card processing (2.7% + $0.30; +1.5% on foreign cards; +1% currency conversion; a further 3% is reported on some access-app listings such as Discord — check before pricing a gated product). About 4–6% on a $20 sale. Its 30% marketplace commission was removed in May 2025, so a marketplace sale costs the same as a direct one.
  • Etsy — 6.5% transaction fee plus payment processing charged separately (3% + $0.25 for US sellers, charged on the total the buyer pays; rates differ for sellers in other countries), plus a $0.20 listing fee. Roughly 11% on a $24 sale, and higher on cheaper ones. ⚠️ The 6.5% is not the whole story — processing is on top.
  • Gumroad — 10% + $0.50 platform, plus processing (~2.9% + $0.30). ⚠️ 30% on a Gumroad Discover (marketplace) sale, not 10%.

Why "all-in %" needs your price: a $24 Gumroad sale pays $2.90 (10% + $0.50) plus about $1.00 processing = ~$3.90, which is ~16%, not the ~13% the percentages alone suggest. On a $100 sale the same fees come to ~13.7%. The fixed charges are the reason.

Course platforms — entry plans

  • Teachable — Starter (~$39/mo) charges a 7.5% transaction fee on top of processing; Builder and Growth charge 0% platform fee.
  • Kajabi — Basic (~$179/mo), 0% platform fee (processing 2.9% + $0.30; a third-party processor costs 5% on Basic).
  • Thinkific — around $54/mo ($40 billed yearly), 0% on paid plans (its 2026 pricing is changing — check the current figure). Skool — $99/mo flat, community-first. Both approximate, not primary-verified.

Checked at source in October 2026; next review due January 2027. This is dated copy, not a live feed — verify against each platform's own pricing page before you rely on a figure.
