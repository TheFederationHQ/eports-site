ETSY - THE SOP INDEX + THE SUSPENSION-RESPONSE PLAN  (Chapter 8)

A business that only runs when you run it is a single point of failure
with good habits. Get the load-bearing tasks out of your head, then
rehearse the one emergency panic is guaranteed to make worse.

  SOP INDEX - the load-bearing few  (written = anyone can run it)

  SUSPENSION-RESPONSE PLAN

  0. VERIFY it is really from Etsy (phishing is common);
     CLASSIFY the restriction: temporary / listing /
     payment / permanent; CHECK what access remains
     (desktop browser? orders? funds? - an account Etsy
      calls SUSPENDED cannot use the seller apps or buy
      Etsy labels; lesser restrictions differ).
  1. FOLLOW the remedy the notice names
  2. GATHER evidence from my backups (records, charter, proof)
  3. If appealing: fix what is fixable, then ONE complete,
     factual reply via Etsy's official channel only
  4. KEEP in-rule: ship what I can (non-Etsy label ready);
     Etsy Messages = order logistics ONLY - never explain,
     vent, or hint at buying elsewhere; my OWN opted-in list
     is where I share honest news (still never diverting a
     live Etsy order)
  5. ACTIVATE the liquidity buffer

  DRILLED (simulated: fake notice, nothing sent): date ______
  What broke in the drill, now fixed: __________________

  Not legal advice and not a magic key: some restrictions lift,
  some appeals succeed and some do not. The plan stops panic making
  it worse and keeps the rest of the business breathing. Follow the
  actual notice and Etsy's CURRENT Help pages over anything here.
