  loaded $/sale = chapter 2's number (all costs, incl. your time)
  TOTAL $ = loaded x units in a fixed window (a rolling 90 days)
  $/hr = TOTAL / the owner-hours the design ate (incl. diffuse time)

SORT by the constraint you named in chapter 1:
  constraint = money?  -> sort by TOTAL loaded $
  constraint = hours?  -> sort by $ / owner-hour  (the mug may jump)

LABEL each — a DIAGNOSIS, not yet an action (read rank BESIDE funnel):
  REACH-LIMITED   few views (its economics may be fine)
  HEALTHY         earns well, funnel intact
  OFFER-LIMITED   views/favorites but few orders
  MARGIN-DEAD     orders but little/no loaded contribution

The action comes LATER, not here: a reach-limited design is a
candidate for ch6 — but only if a channel can hit its CAC ceiling.
A margin-dead still-seller is ch8's call. Diagnose here; decide there.

⚠ Give a young / tiny-volume design a floor (a couple of dozen
sales, or a full window) before you let the table judge it.
