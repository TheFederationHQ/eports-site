YOUR ONE-PAGE DATA RULE
From "AI for Your Business — Basics", chapter 5
================================================================

The rule your whole team follows, so AI stays a help and never becomes the
thing that costs you a fine or a customer. Keep it short enough that people
actually read it. Fill it in, then sit the team down, read it out, and have
everyone who touches these tools -- you included -- put their name to it.

⚠️ Not legal advice. For anything tricky, ask a professional. This is UK data-
protection law (UK GDPR) in plain English; elsewhere the shape is similar, but
check your own.


1 - WHAT WE MAY PUT IN  (your lower-risk list)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Public and yours (website copy, published prices, marketing); your own drafts;
real examples with names and identifying details removed -- removed enough that
no one could be worked out; general "how do I..." questions with no private
data in them.

3 - WHICH ACCOUNT WE USE FOR REAL WORK
----------------------------------------------------------------
Approved business tool (a seat per person, never a shared login): ____________

  [ ] The admin has ACCEPTED the vendor's data-processing terms in the settings
  [ ] "Train on our data" is turned OFF
  [ ] It covers our data leaving the UK  (most tools process it abroad)

(A business plan is the right FOUNDATION -- not a certificate that says you're
compliant. It only helps once these three are actually done; a seat you bought
but never set up is not a shield. You still need a lawful reason to use personal
data, and for anything involving real personal data at scale, get proper advice.)
