YOUR ONE-PAGE DATA RULE
From "AI for Your Business — Basics", chapter 5
================================================================

The rule your whole team follows, so AI stays a help and never becomes the
thing that costs you a fine or a customer. Keep it short enough that people
actually read it. Fill it in, then sit the team down, read it out, and have
everyone who touches these tools -- you included -- put their name to it.

⚠️ Not legal advice. For anything tricky, ask a professional. Written for US
businesses: the FTC holds you to your privacy promises and treats weak data
security as unfair; about 20 states have privacy laws (many small firms are
exempt) and all 50 have data-breach laws; health, financial and children's data
have their own rules. Outside the US (UK/EU GDPR), check your own regulator.
