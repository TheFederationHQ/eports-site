Why trust it

The research, checked

Every figure carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its source, a practitioner's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a disputed figure comes with its range and caveat. The fast-changing bits — platform fees and policies — are dated and re-checked, because the real reason people lose money here is rarely laziness; it's following advice that used to be true.
