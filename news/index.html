Shipping abroad for Christmas? USPS's send-by date is December 9, eight days before the US one

If you ship Christmas orders abroad by USPS Priority Mail International or First-Class Package International, USPS recommends sending them by Wednesday, December 9. That's eight days before its December 17 date for US Ground Advantage. With a 3-day processing time, it points to a last international order day of Friday, December 4, about three weeks from now.

Digital Products desk · 11 Nov 2026

A $15 planner leaves you $13.51, $13.12 or $12.26 after fees, depending on the site

On a $15 digital planner sold to a US buyer by card, Payhip's free plan leaves you about $13.51 after fees, Etsy about $13.12 and Gumroad about $12.26. That gap is smaller than most sellers expect. What changes the answer is where the buyer comes from.

Marketplaces desk · 10 Nov 2026

Etsy's Share & Save pays 6.5% back until January 4. Here's when Offsite Ads cancel it

Until January 4, 2027, Etsy will pay you back 6.5% on eligible orders that come through links you share yourself, so a $45 order earns $2.93 back. That matches Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee, so on those sales the fee is cancelled out. The catch: if the buyer's last click was one of Etsy's Offsite Ads, you get no refund and pay an ad fee on top of the transaction fee.

Marketplaces desk · 9 Nov 2026

A 25% off Etsy sale can cut your profit nearly in half. Here's the Black Friday maths

On a $40 Etsy item, a 25% off sale can drop what you keep from $19.75 to $10.70. Your costs stay the same while the price falls, so profit falls by almost half, and you'd need about 85% more orders to stand still. Before you set a Black Friday discount, work out what each sale will leave you. It takes about two minutes.

Marketplaces desk · 6 Nov 2026

Printful and Printify's $0.40 holiday surcharge: who pays it, and how to cover it

If you sell through Printful or Printify, each order you ship to a US address costs you an extra $0.40 until January 17, 2027. Both suppliers charge it to you, the seller, and neither adds it to your storefront prices automatically. Unless you've raised your prices, it's coming out of your margin on every US order right now. The one exception: Printify orders that use TikTok Shipping.

Marketplaces desk · 5 Nov 2026

TikTok Shop's Black Friday sale runs November 11–30. Here's who can join, and the catch on returns

Holiday sales on TikTok Shop can come back to you until February 10, 2027. That's the return deadline for eligible purchases in TikTok Shop's Black Friday and Cyber Monday campaign, which runs from November 11 to 30, Pacific time. To get your products in, your shop has to meet TikTok's health scores, and most campaign applications take 5 to 14 business days to review. A shop that applies today may only get in partway through the sale, so apply now.

Marketplaces desk · 4 Nov 2026

The EU's new €2 parcel fee is live. A three-item order can now carry €15 in charges

If you ship orders to EU buyers from the US or anywhere else outside the EU, your margin on those orders just shrank. Since November 1, each order carries a €2 handling fee for every distinct item in it, on top of the €3 customs duty the EU added in July. It's counted by item, not by parcel, and it isn't refunded if the buyer sends the order back.

Marketplaces desk · 3 Nov 2026

eBay payouts go on-demand by default from November 4. Here's the setting to check

If you sell on eBay in the US, your sales money may stop landing in your bank on its own once eBay balance is switched on for your account. eBay starts that rollout gradually on Wednesday, November 4, and payouts under eBay balance are on demand by default. If you rely on a daily or weekly payout, check your schedule as soon as eBay tells you the change has reached your account.

Marketplaces desk · 2 Nov 2026

Etsy's new photo rule: one real, unedited picture for most products. Here's who has to fix what

On October 22 Etsy changed its rules on listing photos and AI. Most listings now need at least one photo of the actual item, against a real background, that hasn't been edited at all. Patterns, recipes and instructions written by AI are no longer allowed. If a listing doesn't meet the image rules and a buyer opens a case because the item is materially different, you may be the one paying the refund.
