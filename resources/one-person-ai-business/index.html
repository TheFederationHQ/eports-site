Kept current Model prices & payment fees, checked by hand

The two fastest-changing figures the books touch — AI model prices and payment fees — checked by hand and dated. The books' method doesn't move; these numbers do.

⚠️ A percentage is not the whole fee, and a model tier is not one fixed price. Payment fees add a fixed amount per sale on top of a percentage, and differ by country; model prices change monthly. Always work it out at your numbers — the margin spreadsheet does it for you.

AI model tiers — the shape, not the brand (illustrative)

  • Reasoning tier (most capable, priciest — for judgment) — the frontier "thinking" models, roughly $2–10 in / $10–50 out per million tokens (varying a lot by model) (a token ≈ ¾ of a word).
  • Balanced tier (the workhorse — for most writing) — roughly $0.75–2 in / $4–10 out per million tokens.
  • Fast-cheap tier (bulk and simple jobs) — roughly $0.10–0.30 in / $0.50–2.50 out.

The major model families (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) generally offer all three tiers. A free tier can usually run the whole first loop; upgrade only when a real limit blocks you. Look up the current name and price for each tier at the provider's own pricing page; they move monthly.

Payment routes — per digital sale

  • Raw card processor (e.g. Stripe) — US cards ~2.9% + $0.30 (verified October 2026; premium/commercial and non-US cards cost more, and other countries have their own rates). Keeps you the most of the sale; you handle tax, refunds and chargebacks yourself.
  • Merchant of record (e.g. Lemon Squeezy) — 5% + $0.50, and it handles your sales tax, at home and abroad, for you — a whole job lifted off one pair of hands.
  • All-in stores (e.g. Gumroad) — ⚠️ headline percentage plus a fixed fee, but whether card processing is bundled or charged on top is reported inconsistently, and Gumroad became a full merchant of record in 2025. We print no "you keep" figure for these on purpose — confirm the real take on the platform's own current pricing page before you rely on it.
