### ⚠️ The compliance line — keep it policy-safe
The insert points to your OTHER ETSY listings + an Etsy coupon.
- DO  thank them; give real value; link your Etsy collection
- DON'T  "buy direct from my site", off-Etsy QR, a mailing list
- WHY  Etsy bans using a sale to move the buyer off-platform —
       any medium; a printed card is not a loophole.
