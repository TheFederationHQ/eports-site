The unit is the week, not the item. Block it in the calendar you look at.

=====================================================================
THE BLOCKS (the book's example is about eight hours — shrink the blocks,
not their number)
=====================================================================
SOURCING ....... day ______  hours ____  route (same every week): ________
                 count target ____ items · cash limit $____ (chapter 5)
                 Ends when either target is hit or the time runs out.
PHOTO + LISTING  day ______  hours ____
                 First: every photo, one setup, one go. Then: list from the
                 skeleton. Put two finished listings into the draft bank.
SHIPPING ....... windows: ______ / ______ / ______  (~20 min each)
                 No special trips. Supplies live in one box, restocked in
                 the listing block.
LOG ............ Sunday night, 15 min — one line (below).

=====================================================================
THE LISTING SKELETON (set up once for your category)
=====================================================================
Title slots: brand · what it is · material · size · colour
Description, the same four prompts every time: ___ / ___ / ___ / ___
Item specifics pre-filled: ______________________________________
Shipping, handling and return terms: saved once.

=====================================================================
THE HANDLING RULE
=====================================================================
The handling time I promise must be one my windows can keep.
My windows: ____________  →  handling time I'll promise: ____ business days
(A Friday-evening sale under a one-business-day promise must ship by
Monday — and the platform's ship-by date is the one that counts.)

=====================================================================
THE WORKING FLOAT — a float, not a stockpile
=====================================================================
One listing block's worth: about 8–10 items, no more. Seed it from things
you already own; top it up only from a Saturday that came in OVER target.
Count every item in it as cash on the shelf.

=====================================================================
THE FIRST BAD WEEK
=====================================================================
Run the blocks anyway, smaller. List from the float if sourcing was thin;
post from the bank if the listing block was lost; ship in the windows
regardless. Log it honestly — a thin week on the record is data; a skipped
week is a hole.

=====================================================================
THE LOG — one line a week
=====================================================================
Week | Hours | Sourced | Cost | Listed live | Sold | Cash received | Note
_____|_______|_________|______|_____________|______|_______________|_____
_____|_______|_________|______|_____________|______|_______________|_____
(Cash received = everything that came in for the week's sales, platform
payouts plus any cash from in-person sales.)
