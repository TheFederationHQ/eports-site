TIKTOK SHOP AFFILIATE — THE PRE-POST COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
From TikTok Shop Affiliate — Basics, chapter 4.
Free from ePORTS — eports.global/resources

------------------------------------------------------------
Run this before you post ANY shoppable video. Thirty seconds,
every time — like a seatbelt. If it fails one line, fix it
before the video goes up.

  [ ]  1. DISCLOSURE TOGGLE
          The app's promotional / commercial-content setting
          is switched ON.

  [ ]  2. SAID OUT LOUD
          You mention it's a paid link early in the video,
          not tacked on at the end.

  [ ]  3. ON SCREEN
          A few seconds of text — "paid promotion" or
          "affiliate link" — visible in the video.

  [ ]  4. IN THE CAPTION
          "#ad" or "paid promotion" in the FIRST line, before
          the "...more" cut-off where it stays hidden.

  [ ]  5. EVERY CLAIM IS TRUTHFUL AND SUPPORTABLE
          Based on what you've genuinely seen. Nothing about
          health, money or safety you couldn't back up if
          someone asked.

------------------------------------------------------------
One honest limit: this covers disclosure and claims — the two
things beginners get wrong most — not every rule TikTok has
(restricted products, music rights and the like still apply).
And these exact settings shift as the app updates: treat the
five moves as the goal, and use whatever the current posting
screen calls them.

Handled with confidence, a clear disclosure reads as exactly
that — confidence. Open feels easier to trust than shady.
