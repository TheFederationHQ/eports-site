ePORTS — Print on Demand: the review loop

A quiet first week is normal, not failure — it is data; read it calmly.

MY REVIEW LOOP —
 Review booked for (a date ~2 weeks out): __________
 At the review, read: views · clicks/visits · sales
 Funnel points me to: 1 reach · 2 impression · 3 page · 4 offer
 Change ONE specific thing, then leave it until the next review.

 1 no views  -> get it in front of people (a traffic problem, not a design one)
 2 no clicks -> the main mockup / the title
 3 no buy    -> the page: price, details, trust
 4 the offer -> the design or buyer may be off (rarest — reach for it last)

With tiny numbers none of this is proof — gather a little more evidence before
you act.
