The fine print

Privacy

The short version: the tools on this site run in your browser. They store nothing and send nothing. We only hold your details if you buy something from us, or if we write to you at your work address, and this page says exactly what that means.

In plain English What happens when you use this site

The calculators and dashboards here — the pricing floor, the profit and margin tools, the allocation dashboard — do all of their arithmetic on your own device, inside your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere, not saved between visits, and not seen by us. Close the tab and they are gone.

The worksheets and spreadsheets are ordinary files you download directly. Downloading one tells us nothing about you beyond the ordinary web-server request your browser makes for any page.

The specifics Cookies, tracking, accounts and ads

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no login on this site, so browsing it gives us no name, email or password.
  • No tracking cookies. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Nothing here follows you around the web.
  • No forms. There is no contact form or newsletter box that collects your details on this site.
  • No ads today. These pages carry no advertising at present. If that ever changes, this page will be updated first to say exactly what an ad partner would collect, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it.
  • Ordinary web-host processing. Like almost every website, this site is delivered by a web host that, in the normal course of serving pages, may process standard request data — including your device's network (IP) address, which can count as personal data — under its own privacy policy, which governs what it keeps and for how long. ePORTS itself does not use that data to identify you, build a profile, or track you across sites.

When we do hold your details If you buy, or if we write to you

  • Who we are. ePORTS is run by The Federation Inc., a Wyoming company, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. Email: hello@eports.global. We are the "controller" of the details below. We are based outside the UK and have not appointed a UK data protection representative, because our processing is occasional and low-risk (UK GDPR Article 27(2)). We will review that as sales grow, and name a representative here if we appoint one.
  • Where your details go. Details we hold are stored in the United States, where we are based. Payhip passes your purchase details to us there under its own terms. Besides Payhip, the only others who handle them are our payment provider (PayPal or Stripe), our email provider, and a professional adviser such as an accountant if we need one. We make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.
  • If you buy a book. Checkout and the download are run by Payhip; under Payhip's end-user license, the download itself is supplied through Payhip Limited. Payment goes to our PayPal or Stripe account, and we never see your card details. Payhip sends us your name, email address, what you bought and when. We use that to help if a file fails, to honour our voluntary refund promise, and for our tax records. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (support, refunds, and keeping the tax records we are required to keep). We keep those records for six years. Payhip's own privacy policy covers what Payhip holds.
  • If we email you at work. We sometimes write once to a named person at a limited company, at their work address, about a book relevant to their business. We use your name, work email and company, taken from where we say in that email (for example your company's website). Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in telling businesses about relevant products. After that single email we keep your name, work email and company for 24 months only so we do not email you again, then delete them. It is not a list for a second email. Reply "stop" and we delete them sooner, keeping only a short note for as long as we send marketing email, so we never contact you again.
  • No selling or sharing. We don't sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

Links out, and your rights A last few honest notes

Some pages link to other companies — a print provider, a selling platform, a spreadsheet you open in your own software. Once you follow a link, that company's own privacy terms apply, not this one.

If we hold details about you (above), you can ask to see them, correct them, have them deleted, limit how we use them, or get a copy to take elsewhere. You can object to us using them for our legitimate interests, and you can always tell us to stop contacting you, at any time, with no reason needed. Email hello@eports.global. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
