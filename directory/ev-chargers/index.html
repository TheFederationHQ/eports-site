FAQ

Home charging, answered

What size home EV charger do I need?

Match the charger to your circuit and your car. 32 amps (40-amp circuit) suits cars that charge slowly, 40 amps (50-amp circuit) suits most, and 48 amps hardwired on a 60-amp circuit lets faster-charging cars go full speed. Each car's page here shows which.

Do I need a Level 2 charger?

Not always. A normal outlet adds roughly 3 to 5 miles of range an hour, which covers about 30 to 40 miles a day overnight. Drive more than that, or want a full battery every morning, and Level 2 is worth it.

Where does this data come from?

Range, charge time and efficiency come from the US EPA and Department of Energy's fueleconomy.gov vehicle file. We work out the energy and charging speed from those figures and label them as estimates.
