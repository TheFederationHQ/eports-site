About ePORTS · The author

Drew Halden

Author of the ePORTS guidebooks

Plain-English guides to selling online, with every fee and rule dated and sourced.

What Drew writes

Eight subjects, three levels each

Drew Halden writes the ePORTS e-commerce guidebooks for people who sell, or want to sell, online. ePORTS is an e-commerce news and publishing brand: dated seller news, free fee and profit calculators, and books on the main ways people make money in digital commerce today.

The series covers eight subjects in three levels each, Basics, Intermediate and Advanced, so you can start where you are: Etsy and handmade shops, print on demand, dropshipping, digital products, TikTok Shop affiliate marketing, reselling, AI for your business, and running a one-person AI business.

The approach

No overnight success, no wealth promises

The books start from one idea: you shouldn't have to know how everything works already. There are no overnight-success promises. You get plain-English explanations, worked numbers, and the platform fees and rules that actually apply, each with the date it was checked and a link to the source, so you can verify it rather than trust it.

Turn information into understanding. Understanding into action. And action into opportunity.
