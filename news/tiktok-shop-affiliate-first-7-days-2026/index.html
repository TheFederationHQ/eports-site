TikTok Shop affiliate: who qualifies, and your first-week plan before Black Friday

Black Friday is 10 days away, and TikTok Shop creators earn a cut of every sale their videos drive, with no stock to buy and no shop to run. Here's who qualifies, the follower line you have to cross first, and a plan for your first week once you're approved.

Edited by Drew Halden17 Nov 2026 Sources checked 17 Nov 2026

ShareXFacebookLinkedIn

The short version: On TikTok Shop, you can earn a cut of every sale your videos drive, with no stock, no shop and no shipping. Sellers set that cut anywhere from 1% to 80%. Black Friday is November 27, so starting now puts you in front of the busiest shopping days of the year. You need 1,000 followers to apply, so step one is checking where you stand.

Who this is for: anyone in the US, 18 or over, who'd rather promote products than run a shop. If you already sell your own products, this is how your future creators think.

How the money works

You add a product to your showcase, post a short video or go LIVE with the product link, and earn commission when someone buys through it. The seller sets the rate.

Commission is paid on what the buyer actually paid, minus refunds. A fully refunded order pays you nothing, so honest videos that set the right expectations earn more over time. It isn't paid at once, either: it waits until the buyer's return window has passed.

Here's a made-up example to show the sum. A $25 gift set with a 15% commission pays you $3.75 a sale. If you made 20 sales, that would be $75. Many beginners make nothing in their first month, so treat any early sales as a bonus while you learn.

The line you have to cross first

This is the part most "start today" videos skip. TikTok's Creator Eligibility Policy says you need:

  • At least 1,000 followers, and you apply yourself.
  • To be 18 or older and based in the US.
  • An identity check, which TikTok may ask for after your first commission. Have your government ID ready.

Under 1,000 followers? Your first week is about growing to that line, not selling. Post useful videos in one niche every day, and come back to this plan when you get there.

Your first weeks have training wheels

Creators under 5,000 followers start in a pilot for at least 30 days. During it, you can post:

  • up to 3 shoppable videos a day, and
  • up to 3 shoppable LIVEs a week,

and only for products from shops with a performance score of 95% or higher. You leave the pilot once you have 5,000 followers and 30 days in it. TikTok can lift the limits early for creators who pass a quiz and hit its health and activity targets.

That's not a bad thing. Up to 3 videos a day is more than most beginners manage, and the 95% rule steers you toward well-rated shops.

Your first 7 days, once you're approved

  1. Day 1: apply. In the TikTok app, go to TikTok Studio, then Monetization, then TikTok Shop for Creator. Once you're approved, turn on your showcase.
  2. Day 2: pick one niche you'd happily talk about for a month, like kitchen gadgets, skincare or desk setups. Start with products you already own and can film today. Add 5 to 10 with good reviews.
  3. Days 3 to 6: post up to 3 videos a day. Show the product working in the first 2 seconds. Say who it's for and what it costs. Gift angles work in November. Say clearly that you earn from the link: the FTC expects it, and TikTok has a setting for it.
  4. Day 7: read your numbers. Note which videos got clicks. Four days is early, so keep testing the products that got views, and plan your Black Friday week around them.

Samples: order them now for next week

Samples won't arrive in time for your first videos, so order them now for the week after.

Free samples need a TikTok Shop sale in the last 120 days, and the seller has to approve each one. You then have 14 days from delivery to post a public video, kept up for at least 3 days, or a 10-minute LIVE.

Refundable samples work before your first sale. You buy the product. TikTok refunds you in full, shipping and tax included, once your content hits the seller's sales goal. Those orders must clear a 30-day return period inside your 120 days, so TikTok suggests hitting the goal within 90.

One rule that protects you

If a seller cuts the commission on a product you're already promoting, you keep the old rate for a 30-day locked period, with a warning 2 days before it ends. Take the product out of your showcase and you lose that locked rate. A raise reaches you straight away. So when sellers lift rates for Black Friday, add those products early.

Do this now

  1. Check your follower count. 1,000 or more? Apply today. Under? Start posting daily in one niche.
  2. Run your own numbers in our free affiliate earnings calculator: price, commission and the sales you think you can drive.
  3. Film what you own, and order one refundable sample of your best bet for next week.
  4. Keep a simple record of what you earn. Commission is taxable income.

What's still unknown: how much you'll earn. That depends on your niche, your videos and the commission sellers offer, and it could be nothing at first. Treat your first month as practice.
