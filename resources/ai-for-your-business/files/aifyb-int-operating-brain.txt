======================================================================
  THE OPERATING BRAIN — a template
  AI for Your Business — Intermediate, Chapter 2
======================================================================

Your AI is only ever as good as what it knows about your business. The
operating brain is that knowledge, written down once, in one shared place
the whole team — and the tool — works from, so the output sounds like ONE
company no matter whose hands are on the keyboard.

This is a starting shape. Change the words to fit your business; it's
yours, not a form to file. Keep each section short enough that someone
actually reads it.

======================================================================
  THE RULE THAT MAKES IT REAL (not just a document nobody opens)
======================================================================
Using the brain is not optional — and, the part that makes that stick,
it is CHECKABLE:

  • Every piece of AI-assisted work carries a marker: which version of
    the brain it was built from (e.g. "brain v3, 14 March").

  • Before the work moves on, the approver's FIRST look is provenance —
    not quality yet, just: does it carry the brain marker? No marker, or
    a stale one, and it goes straight back, unread, to be redone from
    the brain.

  • When a thin or out-of-date brain lets a miss through, that's a
    correction — logged and folded back into the master by the custodian,
    so the same miss can't happen twice.

Build it as the TEAM'S, not a memo from you: you draft the skeleton, but
each function's operator fills in their corner (the questions clients
actually ask, the three things juniors get wrong). One master copy, one
custodian, one queue of improvements. Everyone points the tool at the
same copy; nobody keeps a private one.
