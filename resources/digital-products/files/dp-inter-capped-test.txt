THE CAPPED-TEST RULES PAGE
From "Digital Products — Intermediate", chapter 11
================================================================

The point of a paid traffic test is to buy an ANSWER, not sales. It works only
if you write the rules before you spend, when you are calm — not during, when
you are hopeful. Fill this in, then do not deviate from it.


RULE 1 · THE ZERO-COST ROUTE COMES FIRST
----------------------------------------------------------------
Before any ad spend, have you exhausted the free traffic that already works?
  My best free channel: ______________________________
  [ ] Yes, it is running and I have its numbers.  ->  proceed
  [ ] No.  ->  do that first. Paid traffic amplifies a page that converts; it
                cannot rescue one that does not.


RULE 2 · THE HARD CAP  (a number you have decided you can lose)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Total I will spend on this test:   £__________   (£100–£150 is enough to
                                                    learn something)
  I will stop at this number even if it looks like it is "about to work".


RULE 3 · THE STOP-LOSS  (the daily line)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Daily spend:                       £__________
  If after ______ days the numbers are clearly bad, I stop early. I do not
  need to spend the whole cap to get the answer.


RULE 4 · WHAT "IT WORKED" MEANS  (decide NOW)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Read the result off your scorecard's CONTRIBUTION per visitor, not revenue per
visitor (revenue still has the platform's cut inside it).

  Contribution per visitor from chapter 9:  £__________
  Cost per visitor I am paying:              £__________

  [ ] Cost per visitor is BELOW my contribution per visitor  ->  it works;
      you can consider spending more, carefully.
  [ ] Cost per visitor is ABOVE it  ->  it does not, at this price. You have
      bought a real answer for the cost of the cap. That is a good use of it.


RULE 5 · THE HONEST LIMIT
----------------------------------------------------------------
A £150 test tells you about £150 of traffic. It is a probe, not a verdict on
the whole channel. Treat a clear "no" as final and a marginal "maybe" as a
reason to fix the page, not to spend more.
