From "The One-Person AI Business — Advanced", Chapter 3
================================================================

Positioning is not marketing and not what you SAY about yourself. It's a claim
to a specific corner — the one thing you're the trusted choice for, for a
specific customer — that the MARKET'S OWN BEHAVIOR proves. You don't win it
with a tagline or by declaring you're "the best" (that's just authority). You
win it by narrowing to a corner you can own, checking whether people will pay for it,
and publishing the give-first proof only your work could make.
