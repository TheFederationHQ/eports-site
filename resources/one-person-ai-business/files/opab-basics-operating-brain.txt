THE STARTER OPERATING BRAIN
From "The One-Person AI Business — Basics", Chapter 3
================================================================

A generic AI assistant sounds like everyone, which means it sounds like no
one, which means it does not sell. The fix is not a better model — it is
CONTEXT. This half-page is that context. Paste it at the top of any prompt and
a clever stranger becomes staff who have read your handbook.

⚠️ This is the STARTER brain — half a page, on purpose. The full operating
brain (decision rules, per-function standards, governance) is the Intermediate
book. Do not build that now. Half a page. Enough to sound like you. Move on.


1 · WHO YOU SERVE  (one paragraph)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The one narrow person. Their situation, their recurring annoyance, and the
words THEY use for it (not the words you use).

  ______________________________________________________________

2 · WHAT YOU SELL  (one paragraph)

Your model and rough offer. Even if it changes, write today's.

  ______________________________________________________________


3 · HOW YOU SOUND
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Three words for your voice (e.g. "plain, dry, no hype"): ____________
  One line you would NEVER write (e.g. "no 'unlock your potential'"): ________


4 · WHAT YOU WILL NOT DO  (your lines)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] No fake scarcity.
  [ ] No promising outcomes I can't control.
  [ ] No claiming a person or brand endorsed me when they didn't.
  [ ] ____________________________________________________________


5 · THE FACTS THAT DON'T CHANGE WEEKLY
----------------------------------------------------------------
  My price: $______   My one promise: ______________________________
  My refund stance: ____________________________________________


THE TEST
----------------------------------------------------------------
Paste this in and ask your writing engine to introduce your business in three
sentences. If it sounds like YOU, the brain works. If it sounds generic, it is
too vague — add the specific words your buyer uses, and try again. That loop
IS the skill.
