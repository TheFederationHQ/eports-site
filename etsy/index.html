The series

Etsy, mastered as a platform

Most Etsy advice is about the product. The thing that decides whether you make money is the platform — its search, its fees, its rules, its risks. This series is that. Every book comes with its free worksheets and the take-home calculator.

Etsy looks like your shop, but you are renting a corner of someone else's marketplace — and the landlord makes every rule that matters. This series is the honest version: what Etsy actually leaves you after its fees, the two ways to keep an account alive, how its search really works, and how to stop hoping to be found and start being found on purpose. You have already met the method — the free worksheets and calculator are lifted straight from these pages. The books are the why behind them. (Making the product itself — digital downloads or print-on-demand designs — has its own ePORTS books; here, the platform is the subject.)
