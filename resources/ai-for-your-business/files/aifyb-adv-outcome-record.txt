THE GOVERNED OUTCOME RECORD
From "AI for Your Business — Advanced", chapter 2
================================================================

Your archive is a record of what HAPPENED. What you need is a record of what
WORKED. They look almost identical and they are not the same thing at all.

Every business records what happened -- the law insists on it. Almost nobody
records the judgment that sat on top: did that turn out right? A rival can buy
your tools this afternoon and, given a year, accumulate the same volume of
what-happened. What they cannot get at any price is your what-worked, because
the only way to produce it is to have done the work and formed the judgment.

Set this up in under an hour. If it takes longer, you have built the wrong
thing. There is no database here, nothing to migrate, no archive to clean.

⚠️ DO NOT BACKFILL. Reconstructed judgments are guesses wearing the clothes of
evidence. Start Monday with entry one.


1 - NAME THE ASSET
----------------------------------------------------------------
When your selected edge works, what is it drawing on that a rival hasn't got?
Not the tool. Not the people exactly -- people leave. The thing underneath
that accumulates.

  The thing underneath my edge that a rival hasn't got is

  ______________________________________________________________

⚠️ If that sentence contains the name of a piece of software, it is wrong. Go
one level down and ask "and what's underneath that?" until you stop reaching
for a tool.

If you're not sure, find your candidate in this table:

  YOU SELECTED                  THE ASSET IS USUALLY
  ---------------------------   -----------------------------------------
  A record only you have        which of your judgments proved right --
                                not the files themselves
  Decisions that compound       the allocation or call itself, and what
                                followed it
  Switching costs you've        which commitments you actually met, and
  earned                        where you failed
  An offering only you can      the method -- which approach settled which
  make                          kind of problem


2 - DEFINE WHAT "WORKED" MEANS  (before you capture anything)
----------------------------------------------------------------
If you don't write this, five people apply five private standards and after
three months you have a record you can't trust. It must be OBSERVABLE --
checkable by a second person, from records that already exist.

  "Worked" means: ______________________________________________

  ______________________________________________________________

  Good:  "the client acted on it without needing a second explanation"
         "the job closed on the first visit, inside the window we promised"
  Bad:   "the client was happy"  (nobody can check it)

Read it aloud to whoever will be recording. If they say "well, what about
when...", that's your first exception, not an objection. Log it and move on.


3 - THE TWO MOMENTS  (both must already exist)
----------------------------------------------------------------
What you judged and what actually happened almost never occur at the same
time. So capture at two points -- both of them moments the work already pauses.

  THE DECISION MOMENT (fields 1-3, typed by the person who just made the call,
  while they still hold it in their head. A judgment reconstructed a week later
  is a memory of one.)

  Ours is: ______________________________________________________

  THE OUTCOME MOMENT (field 4 -- bolt it onto a pause that already exists: the
  job marked closed, the file archived, the invoice going out.)

  Ours is: ______________________________________________________

⚠️ The most common way this dies is a NEW ritual -- a Friday review, a weekly
log, a form somebody has to remember. That survives about four weeks. If you
can't find an existing moment for either half, that's a signal about the edge,
not about the record.


4 - FOUR FIELDS -- AND NO FIFTH ADDED IN THE MOMENT
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The situation, in a few words
  2. What we did
  3. What we judged at the time
  4. What actually happened          <-- the outcome link. THIS is the asset.

Every field past four cuts compliance, and a record kept 40% of the time is
worse than useless -- you can't tell whether the missing entries are the
failures.

  Worked example, two firms:

  SITUATION      first-year company,      | boiler swap, tight attic
                 shareholder loan         | access, Tuesday
  WHAT WE DID    sent the standard        | sent Danny, booked 2 hrs,
                 explainer with figures   | took the long ladder
  WHAT WE        "clear enough -- they've | "he's done three like it,
  JUDGED         had one before"          | should be one visit"
  WHAT ACTUALLY  came back with two       | closed first visit,
  HAPPENED       questions on the balance | out by 3.40

Two sentences at the decision, one at the close. That is the whole instrument.

The fields are not frozen forever -- you will find something missing, and you
should. But they change ONLY AT A REVISION, deliberately, with the authority of
the rule from chapter 3. Never mid-quarter because somebody had a good idea on
a Tuesday: change the instrument halfway through and you cannot compare
anything either side of the change.


5 - FOUR ROLES, FOUR PEOPLE
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is where firms quietly cheat, and the roles are what turn a personal habit
into something the business owns.

  CANDIDATE OWNER  ______________________  owns whether the edge works.
                                           Does NOT record and does NOT check
                                           -- nobody marks their own homework.

  RECORDER         ______________________  does the work, makes the call, logs
                                           at the decision moment, and CLOSES
                                           OUT field 4 at the outcome moment.

  CHECKER          ______________________  no stake in the answer. Samples
                   sampling ____ per ____  against the written definition, and
                   first sampling date:    looks for entries that never got
                   ______________________  closed. An orphaned row -- a
                                           judgment with no outcome -- is what
                                           kills this quietly.

  YOU              owns none of it, reads the exception log.

⚠️ Four roles is why a one-person business cannot do this. That isn't a boast -- it is
the reason a small team can build something a solo competitor can't.


6 - EXCEPTIONS: LOG THEM, DON'T ARGUE THEM
----------------------------------------------------------------
When a case doesn't fit the definition, log it as an exception rather than
forcing it into a box. Nobody rewrites the definition on the spot -- changing
the yardstick mid-quarter destroys the comparison. Log it, leave it, move on.
The authority to revise belongs to the rule you write in chapter 3.


7 - THE CAPTURE BOUNDARY  (close chapter 1's permissions question)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Chapter 1 gave your candidate a status. Now write the boundary -- the specific
fields you may record -- in one line the recorder can actually follow.

  Status:   [ ] clear   [ ] irrelevant   [ ] restricted pending advice

  WE MAY RECORD: ______________________________________________

  WE MAY NOT RECORD: __________________________________________

  Routed to (if restricted): __________  Expected back: __________

  How long we keep it: __________  Who may see it: _____________

Three routes exist where client material is involved: ASK properly (not
boilerplate buried in the small print); AMEND at renewal; or RING-FENCE --
record only what is unambiguously yours and name what's excluded.

⚠️ Where the status is restricted, capture starts ONLY on the fields you have
explicitly listed as approved above -- never on "everything except" the excluded
list. If a field isn't on the approved list, it isn't captured. Name the
excluded material explicitly too, so nobody has to guess on a Tuesday afternoon.

⚠️ Not legal advice. Your professional body, your contracts and your own
adviser decide what binds you. This sheet only asks whether you could SHOW
someone, in writing, what you're allowed to reuse.


8 - START DATE
----------------------------------------------------------------
  We start (forward-only, no backfill): ________________________

By your go/no-go date you'll have somewhere around 150 judged outcomes.
That is not a training set and was never meant to be. It is enough for one
specific job: enough for a person to see one repeating pattern clearly enough
to write a rule from it. A hundred cases somebody actually judged will show you
more than ten thousand nobody did -- because the ten thousand don't say which
ones went well.
