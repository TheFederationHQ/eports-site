DROPSHIPPING - THE SINGLE-POINTS-OF-FAILURE AUDIT  (Chapter 12)

Five things that can stop a store your size. You own one of them - the
last. A risk with a date is a plan; a risk without one is a hope.

  1. PAYMENT PROCESSOR - business stops within the hour
     Reduction: a second processor approved and configured.   (days)
  2. AD ACCOUNT - traffic stops immediately
     Reduction: verification done + one channel you own.     (weeks)
  3. SUPPLIER - fulfillment stops while spend continues
     Reduction: a second supplier quoted and trialed.         (weeks)
  4. PLATFORM - the rules change under you
     Reduction: own the customer list and the name.          (months)
  5. YOU - service collapses in about a week
     Reduction: written procedures + one person who read them. (weeks)
