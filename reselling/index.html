The fee and tax facts the books rely on

Marketplace fees and tax thresholds change faster than a book can, so these are re-checked at each source.

  • Same
    eBay (US, most categories): 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500, plus $0.40 an order over $10 ($0.30 at $10 or less).eBay selling fees, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
  • Same
    eBay listings: 250 free a month, then $0.35 a listing in most categories, for a seller without a Store.eBay selling fees, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
  • Same
    Mercari: 10% of the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays, with no separate processing fee, on listings created or updated since 6 January 2025.Fees on Mercari, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
  • Same
    Form 1099-K from a marketplace or payment app: over $20,000 and more than 200 transactions in a year — though a platform may send one for less.IRS: Understanding your Form 1099-K, re-checked 19 Oct 2026

Why trust it

The research, checked

Every figure carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its source, a practitioner's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a rule of thumb is called what it is. The fast-changing bits — platform fees, the tax thresholds — are dated and sent to the platform's own page, because they move faster than a book can. And the honesty runs deeper than facts: money here is the target you work toward, never the proof you've made it, because a sale arrives on the buyer's clock. No hype, no borrowed screenshots — the research, checked.
