Marketplaces desk · 2 Nov 2026

Etsy's new photo rule: one real, unedited picture for most products. Here's who has to fix what

On October 22 Etsy changed its rules on listing photos and AI. Most listings now need at least one photo of the actual item, against a real background, that hasn't been edited at all. Patterns, recipes and instructions written by AI are no longer allowed. If a listing doesn't meet the image rules and a buyer opens a case because the item is materially different, you may be the one paying the refund.

What changed for online sellers in 2026: the worries, the wins, and who it hits

If you sell on Etsy, eBay or Mercari, the main fee you pay when something sells is the same today as it was in January. What moved this year is everything around the sale. USPS changed its prices four times, Europe added new charges on parcels, eBay changes how you get paid next week, and Etsy rewrote its photo rules.
