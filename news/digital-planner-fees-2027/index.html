A $15 planner leaves you $13.51, $13.12 or $12.26 after fees, depending on the site

On a $15 digital planner, Payhip's free plan leaves you $13.51, Etsy $13.12 and Gumroad $12.26. But the cheapest fee isn't always the best deal, because the fee also decides who finds your buyers. Here's what each site keeps, and how to pick.

Edited by Drew Halden11 Nov 2026 Sources checked 11 Nov 2026

ShareXFacebookLinkedIn

The short version: On a $15 digital planner sold to a US buyer by card, Payhip's free plan leaves you about $13.51 after fees, Etsy about $13.12 and Gumroad about $12.26. That gap is smaller than most sellers expect. What changes the answer is where the buyer comes from.

Who can ignore this: sellers of printed planners. This is about downloads, where there's nothing to print or post, so fees are one of your biggest costs.

The part most sellers miss: who brings the buyer

Each site charges differently depending on how the buyer found you. A sale from your own link, a sale from the site's own search, and a sale from an ad click can leave you very different amounts.

These are approximate amounts after fees, before income tax, subscriptions and your own costs. Our example is one $15 planner, bought by card from a US buyer, with no discount code. Sales tax is left out.

Two things stand out. Until January 4, Etsy is the cheapest of the three for sales from your own links, thanks to its Share & Save holiday refund, if you're enrolled. And Gumroad's marketplace and Etsy's Offsite Ads both cost far more than a sale you bring yourself.

What Etsy keeps

Etsy charges three fees on that sale:

  • Listing fee: $0.20. On a digital listing, it's charged again each time the listing sells and renews.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5% of the price, so $0.98.
  • Payment processing: 3% plus $0.25, so $0.70.

That's $1.88 in fees. You keep $13.12. Etsy also collects US sales tax for you.

If the buyer came through your own Share & Save link, Etsy refunds the whole 6.5% transaction fee until 9am Eastern on January 4. That's $0.98 back, so you keep $14.10.

If the buyer clicked one of Etsy's Offsite Ads and bought within 30 days, Etsy adds a 15% ad fee. On a $15 planner, that's $2.25, leaving you $10.87.

Once a shop reaches $10,000 in sales over 365 days, the ad rate drops to 12%, and the ads become compulsory for good. Below that, you can opt out.

What Gumroad keeps

Gumroad charges 10% plus $0.50 on sales through your profile or your own links. On $15, that's $2.00.

Gumroad's help page says card processing comes on top, at 2.9% plus $0.30. That's $0.74. PayPal payments carry PayPal's own fees instead, which the page doesn't list.

That's $2.74 in fees. You keep $12.26. In return, Gumroad handles sales tax for you worldwide.

A buyer who finds you through Gumroad's Discover marketplace costs a flat 30%, which includes processing. That's $4.50, leaving you $10.50. Big sellers get a break: once a month's paid sales reach $20,000, new direct sales that month cost 5% plus $0.50.

What Payhip keeps

Payhip's free plan takes 5% of each sale, which is $0.75 on a $15 planner. Stripe or PayPal then charge their own standard rates. At Stripe's US card rate, that's another $0.74.

That's $1.49 in fees. You keep $13.51. Payhip also collects EU and UK VAT, and US sales tax, for you.

Payhip is a store builder rather than a marketplace, so every buyer comes through your own links.

When a paid Payhip plan pays

Payhip's Plus plan costs $29 a month and cuts the fee to 2%. That saves 3% of every sale, so it pays for itself at about $967 a month in sales. At $15 each, that's about 65 planners.

The Pro plan costs $99 a month with no Payhip fee. Compared with the free plan, it pays once you sell about $1,980 a month. It only beats Plus once you sell about $3,500 a month.

What to do this week

  1. Price your planner on all three using the sums above, with your own price.
  2. Count where last year's buyers came from: your links, the site's search, or ads. Etsy's Stats page shows traffic sources. Weigh the fee against the sales each source actually brought you.
  3. Join Etsy's Share & Save before your busiest weeks if you sell on Etsy and share your own links.
  4. Check your Offsite Ads setting in Etsy's Shop Manager. Shops under $10,000 in yearly sales can opt out.
  5. Run your Etsy numbers in our free Etsy fee calculator.

What's still unknown: how much extra traffic each site sends a new planner shop. That depends on your listings, and your own sales history is the best guide.
