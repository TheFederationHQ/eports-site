Why trust it

The research, checked

Everything in these books carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its date and source, a practitioner's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a band or rule of thumb is called what it is — a starting point you replace with your own data. Platform fees are dated and re-checked, because they change faster than a book can. That is the whole promise: not hype, and not somebody's "secret" — the ecommerce research, checked, and handed to you in a form you can act on.
