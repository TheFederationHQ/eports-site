Why trust it

The research, checked

Every figure carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its source and date, a real operator's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a rule of thumb is called what it is — a starting point you replace with your own data. Where a number could not be verified cleanly, the book says so rather than round it into false confidence. Payment fees are dated and re-checked, because they change faster than a book can. No invented case studies, no "our data shows". That is the whole promise: the research, checked, and handed to you in a form you can act on.
