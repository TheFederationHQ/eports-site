Marketplaces

  • No change
    Etsy: 6.5% transaction fee and $0.20 to list.Etsy Fees & Payments Policy, checked 12 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Etsy payment processing (US): 3% + $0.25 charged separately from the transaction fee.Etsy Payments Policy, checked 12 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Etsy Offsite Ads: 15% of an attributed order, capped at $100; 12% once a shop makes $10,000 in sales in any 365 days.Etsy Fees & Payments Policy and Advertising & Marketing Policy, checked 12 Oct 2026 and 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    eBay (US, most categories): 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500, plus $0.40 an order over $10 ($0.30 at $10 or less).eBay selling fees, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    eBay listings: 250 free a month, then $0.35 a listing in most categories, for a seller without a Store.eBay selling fees, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Mercari: 10% of the item price plus any shipping the buyer pays, with no separate processing fee, on listings created or updated since 6 January 2025.Fees on Mercari, checked 19 Oct 2026

Online stores and card payments

  • No change
    Shopify Basic: $39 a month or $29 a month billed yearly.Shopify pricing, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Card payments: 2.9% + 30¢ the US online standard card rate on Shopify Payments (Basic plan), and Stripe's standard rate for US cards.Shopify pricing and Stripe pricing, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Lemon Squeezy: 5% + 50¢ per sale as a merchant of record that handles sales tax for you; some payment methods and international sales cost a little more.Lemon Squeezy pricing, checked 19 Oct 2026

Digital products and courses

  • No change
    Gumroad: 10% + $0.50 on a sale through your own link, plus card processing (2.9% + $0.30); a flat 30%, processing included, when a new customer finds you through Gumroad's Discover marketplace.Gumroad pricing and Gumroad's fees, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Whop: no platform fee on its fee page — cards cost 2.7% + $0.30, plus 1.5% on international cards and 1% if currency is converted.Whop fees, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    Teachable Starter: $39 a month with a 7.5% transaction fee ($29 a month billed yearly); Builder is $89 a month with 0%.Teachable pricing, checked 19 Oct 2026

Print on demand

AI tools for business

  • No change

  • No change

Tax and disclosure rules

  • No change
    Form 1099-K from a marketplace or payment app: over $20,000 and more than 200 transactions in a year — though a platform may send one for less.IRS: Understanding your Form 1099-K, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    US: disclose any commission or free product, clearly. In a video, the disclosure goes in the video itself, not just the description.FTC: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    US: a platform's built-in tag may not be enough. The FTC says not to assume the platform's tool is good enough, and to consider using it alongside your own clear disclosure.FTC: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers, checked 19 Oct 2026
  • No change
    UK: affiliate posts must be obviously identifiable as ads (CAP Code rule 2.1).ASA: Online affiliate marketing, checked 19 Oct 2026
