Advanced

Turn AI into an advantage rivals can't easily copy

For: your operation runs on AI and it's plateaued as an advantage — because the same tools are for sale to everyone, so efficiency alone no longer sets you apart.

You finish with: one AI advantage deployed that is materially harder to copy — it depends on an asset you own — beating a baseline you registered before you could see the answer, running without you or the person who owns it, under a contracted commitment to a paying client, and past a completed dependency and durability audit — or a precise diagnosis of which edge failed to materialize, and the constraint behind it.

Eleven chapters. It opens by taking something away: the evidence says a firm your size will not build a data moat, so chapter one is a selection gate that eliminates rather than a list that inspires — four candidate edges in, one out, with a measured baseline and a dated go/no-go. Then five layers built on that one choice: the record of what worked rather than what happened; one recurring decision bound to a rule that sharpens every quarter; a priced pilot to a single real client; a controlled test of what it's actually worth, measured in margin per delivery hour; and the reliability standard clients reorganize their own week around. Then governed autonomy at scale, the reckoning — a durability audit of how much of this is really yours — the decision it forces, the deploy of one owned-asset advantage under a contracted commitment to a named client, and the quarterly review that keeps the edge compounding.

Comes with, free: the edge selection gate, the governed outcome record, the decision-rule card, the pilot-and-pricing test, and the durability audit with its quarterly review.

Why trust it

The research, checked

Every figure carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its source and date, a real operator's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a rule of thumb is called what it is — a starting point you replace with your own data. The cases are real, named firms with their sources listed, not invented studies or "our data shows". Where a number couldn't be verified cleanly, the book says so rather than round it into false confidence. AI tool prices are dated and re-checked, because they change faster than a book can. That's the whole promise: the research, checked, and handed to you in a form you can act on.
