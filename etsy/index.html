The Etsy series · 3 books

Etsy, mastered as a platform

Most Etsy advice is about the product. The thing that decides whether you make money is the platform — its search, its fees, its rules, its risks. This series is that.

No email, no sign-up.

Etsy looks like your shop, but you are renting a corner of someone else's marketplace — and the landlord makes every rule that matters. This series is the honest version: what Etsy actually leaves you after its fees, the two ways to keep an account alive, how its search really works, and how to stop hoping to be found and start being found on purpose.

You may have already met the method: the free worksheets and calculator are lifted straight from these books, and the books are the why behind them. Making the product itself (digital downloads or print-on-demand designs) has its own ePORTS books; here, the platform is the subject.

Why trust it

The research, checked

Every figure carries the status of its evidence: a checkable fact is named with its source and date, a real operator's own number is flagged as a report rather than a rule, and a rule of thumb is called what it is — a starting point you replace with your own data. No invented statistics, no "our data shows". That is the whole promise: the Etsy research, checked, and handed to you in a form you can act on.
