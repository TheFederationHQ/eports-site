The disclosure rules the books rely on

Commission links are advertising in law, so these are read at the regulators' own pages.

  • Same
    US: disclose any commission or free product, clearly. In a video, the disclosure goes in the video itself, not just the description.FTC: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
  • Same
    US: a platform's built-in tag may not be enough. The FTC says not to assume the platform's tool is good enough, and to consider using it alongside your own clear disclosure.FTC: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
  • Same
    UK: affiliate posts must be obviously identifiable as ads (CAP Code rule 2.1).ASA: Online affiliate marketing, re-checked 19 Oct 2026
