Kept current Base costs & platform fees, checked by hand

The figures the book uses for the reader's arithmetic — the fastest-changing thing in this business — checked by hand and dated below.

⚠️ A percentage is not the whole fee, and a base cost is not one fixed number. Fees add a fixed amount per sale, and base cost moves with the blank, printer and region. Always work it out at your numbers — the calculators do it for you.

Print providers — base cost, a basic tee (illustrative snapshots)

  • Printify — a basic tee often lands near $8–10 on the free plan; a network, so the base cost, quality and shipping change with the printer you pick. Usually the cheapest.
  • Printful — a tee nearer $11–12, for a more consistent finish and branding options. More a brand play than a margin play.
  • Gelato — in between; aims to print locally in many countries, so orders can arrive faster and skip customs within a region.

Figures are snapshots — look up your exact blank, printer and region at the provider's own product page, because they move.

Selling platform fees — Etsy, per sale

  • Etsy — 6.5% transaction fee plus payment processing charged separately, at 3% + $0.25 in the US (other countries set their own), plus a $0.20 listing fee and, in some countries, a small regulatory operating fee. Roughly 10% all-in on a small sale, nearer 12–13% when Offsite Ads orders are averaged in. ⚠️ The 6.5% is not the whole story — processing is on top.

Checked at source 2026-10-12; next review due Jan 2027. Dated copy, not a live feed — verify at each provider's and Etsy's own pricing pages before you rely on a figure. Nothing here is legal advice.
