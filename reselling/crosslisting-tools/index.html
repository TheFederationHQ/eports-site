Crosslisting tools compared: Crosslist, Vendoo and List Perfectly

What each one costs, which plans end your other listings when something sells, and when you don't need one yet. Prices from each tool's own pricing page, checked 12 November 2026.

First Do you need one yet?

Probably not, if you're cross-listing fewer than a few dozen items. Our Reselling — Intermediate book has you cross-list a batch of ten by hand, with one rule: end the other listing the same moment one sells. That costs nothing, and it shows whether a second platform sells your kind of item at all. Read the batch on a date you set in advance. Only if the second platform earns its place, and copying listings starts eating your evenings, is a tool worth paying for.

What a tool buys you is time and a smaller oversell window. It copies one listing to several marketplaces, and on the right plan it ends the other listings automatically when one sells. No tool closes that window completely. Sales can still land in the minutes before it reacts.

Which suits whom Match the tool to how you sell

Crosslist

It lists to eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop, Etsy, Facebook Marketplace, Grailed, Whatnot, Shopify, WooCommerce and OfferUp, according to its own help pages. Its plans differ mainly in how many new items you can add each month (200 on Bronze up to unlimited on Diamond). Automatic delisting is on Gold and Diamond only, so budget $39.99 a month if that's the reason you're buying. On eBay, Etsy, Shopify, Depop, WooCommerce and US Facebook Marketplace it works without your computer on. On Poshmark and the others, it only works while your desktop browser is running Crosslist's extension. Crosslist is for sellers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, not the EU. We earn a commission if you subscribe through this link, and it doesn't change what we recommend: see Crosslist's plans (15% off your first month through our link).

Vendoo

Its cheapest plan lists automatic delisting, and it's the only one of the three with a free trial. If you want to test a tool before paying, that matters. Vendoo's pricing page.

List Perfectly

It's built around a central catalogue you list from. Automatic delisting is only on its top plan, so it suits larger shops that need its other tools. List Perfectly's pricing page.

Free first What to do before you pay anything

  1. Run one sale through our fee comparison calculator to see what each platform leaves you.
  2. Cross-list ten items by hand, at the same price on both platforms, with each platform's floor written down.
  3. End the other listing the moment one sells. If two buyers do get one item, cancel with the true reason, refund promptly and apologise.
  4. Read the batch on the date you set. Only then decide whether a tool is worth its monthly price.
