# ePORTS — Print on Demand: Intermediate
## FOUND ON PURPOSE — the keyword harvest (chapter 5)

Etsy search **matches then ranks** — it is not word-containment.
Harvest real buyer language from four sources, then build the
listing from it, and PUBLISH (a draft can't be found or measured).

### 1. Harvest — pull real phrases from four sources, not guesses
- **Etsy autocomplete** — type your buyer's words; the suggestions
  are the *language of real queries* (not proof of volume).
- **Your own Shop Stats** — the search terms that already brought
  visitors to your shop; and **Marketplace Insights** for real search
  *frequency* (free to every seller with a weekly cap, unlimited on
  Etsy Plus).
- **Competitors' titles & categories** — the words shops already
  selling to your buyer use. (Their TAGS aren't natively visible;
  their titles and categories are.)
- **Your buyer's own words** — reviews and messages.
