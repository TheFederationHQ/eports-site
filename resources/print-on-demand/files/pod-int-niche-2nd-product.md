### Two safety checks — run on the new design before you list it
- [ ] **Test A — others' IP.** No one else's names, art, logos,
      lyrics or characters. It is your original work.
- [ ] **Test B — Etsy's Creativity Standards.** Your own design
      (a purchased template/bundle does not qualify, even licensed);
      AI made with your own prompt is allowed but must be disclosed;
      your production partner is named.

**Build it, list it — that is the win.** A second product for the
