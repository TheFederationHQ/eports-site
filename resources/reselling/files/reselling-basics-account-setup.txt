RESELLING — BASICS · eBAY ACCOUNT SETUP (chapter 2)
Free from ePORTS — eports.global/resources/reselling
About ten minutes. Three settings and one search. Don't list anything yet.

[ ] 1. LINK YOUR PAYOUT
       Connect the checking account eBay pays into and finish the identity
       check. You can't hold a minimum-net rule if you can't watch the
       money land.

[ ] 2. RETURNS: 30 DAYS, BUYER PAYS RETURN SHIPPING
       The safe default for used goods. eBay's Money Back Guarantee still
       covers a genuinely not-as-described item whatever you pick — so "no
       returns" blocks only change-of-mind returns, and it scares off more
       buyers than that saves.

[ ] 3. SHIP-FROM LOCATION + A 1-BUSINESS-DAY HANDLING TIME
       The location lets eBay calculate real shipping for you. One-day
       handling is a promise you can keep.

[ ] 4. FIND THE SOLD FILTER AND RUN ONE SEARCH
       Search for anything in your house, tick "Sold", and just look.
       You're only confirming you can find the tool the next chapter runs on.

Today's deliverable: an account that can take money, and your own eyes on a
real Sold screen. No listing until you can price.
