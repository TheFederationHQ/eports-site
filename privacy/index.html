The fine print, in plain English

Privacy

The short version: the tools on this site run in your browser. They store nothing and send nothing. Below: what our web host logs when you visit, and the details we handle if you buy something, email us, or get a work email from us.

Effective 12 Nov 2026

At a glance

Tools run on your device

The calculators do their sums inside your browser. Close the tab and the numbers are gone.

No accounts. One optional email list

You never need to sign up to use the site. If you choose to join our email list, we get only the email address you give us.

No ad cookies. Analytics only with a yes

No advertising cookies. Google Analytics counts visits with its own cookies, and in Europe only if you say yes. Nothing here follows you around the web for ads.

Never sold

If you buy, we hold only what we need, and we never sell or share it for marketing.

In plain English

What happens when you use this site

The calculators and dashboards here — the pricing floor, the profit and margin tools, the allocation dashboard — do all of their arithmetic on your own device, inside your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere, not saved between visits, and not seen by us. Close the tab and they are gone.

The worksheets and spreadsheets are ordinary files you download directly. Downloading one tells us nothing about you. Like any page, it is an ordinary request to our web host, which is covered below.

The specifics

Cookies, tracking, accounts and ads

No accounts
There is nothing to sign up for and no login on this site, so browsing it gives us no name, email or password.
Cookies
The site sets no advertising cookies. Two of our visit counters (below) work without cookies. The third, Google Analytics, sets its own first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*, kept up to 2 years) and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, only after you press “Allow”. Nothing here follows you around the web for advertising.
"Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control
If your browser sends either signal, neither Umami nor Google Analytics runs for you at all. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of any “sale” or “sharing” under US state privacy laws.
One optional form
The only form on this site is the email sign-up box on our newsletter page. Using it is optional; see If you join our email list below. There is no contact form.
No ads today
These pages carry no advertising at present. In November 2026 we applied to Google AdSense so that, once approved, some news articles can show ads. Applying adds only a small Google ownership tag and our ads.txt file, which load nothing and set no cookies. Before any ad appears, this page will be updated to say exactly what Google and its partners collect, and visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent first.
What our web host logs
This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. When you visit, Cloudflare's network handles the request, and in doing so processes your device's network (IP) address, the page you asked for, the page you came from and your browser type, to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. That is Cloudflare's processing, under Cloudflare's privacy policy, which governs what it keeps and for how long. ePORTS does not receive those logs, and does not use visitor data to identify you, build a profile, or track you across sites. The numbers you type into a calculator are never part of it: they stay on your device.
How we count visits
Since 28 Oct 2026 we use two cookie-free counters so we know which pages are useful. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, the site you came from, your country, device type and browser, and how fast pages load. Umami counts the same kind of page views, plus which store you head to when you click a link to Amazon, Etsy, our shop on Payhip or Crosslist (only the store and the page you clicked from, never anything you type). Neither sets cookies, neither follows you to other websites, and we see only totals, never a list of visitors. The links themselves are not changed, so the store sees the same link you clicked. The numbers you type into a calculator are never counted. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal, Umami does not count you at all, and you can switch it off here too.

We also use Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC, USA, as our service provider) to count visits, pages read and where visitors come from. It sets first-party cookies and Google processes your IP address to estimate your country; IP addresses are not logged or stored in Google Analytics. Analytics data is kept for 14 months at most. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland it stores nothing until you press “Allow” on the small banner; our lawful basis there is your consent. Elsewhere it runs unless you opt out. Advertising features, ad personalisation and Google signals are off. The switch below turns off Umami and Google Analytics together in this browser.
"Was this page useful?"
Since 13 Nov 2026 every page ends with a short question. Your Yes or No and anything you type in the suggestion box are sent to Umami with the address of the page, the same cookie-free way as the visit counts above, so we can see which pages to fix. It is not linked to you, so please leave out your name, email or anything personal. If counting is switched off in your browser, nothing is sent and the box offers to email your suggestion instead.
We do not sell or share your information
We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising, and there are no ad partners. Google receives Analytics data as our service provider and we have turned off its advertising features and data sharing for ads. To opt out of all counting, use the switch above, send a Global Privacy Control signal, or install Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. If any of this changes, this page will say so before it starts.

When we do hold your details

If you buy, join our list, or we write to you

Who we are

ePORTS LLC A Wyoming limited liability company 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA Email: hello@eports.global

ePORTS is run by ePORTS LLC. We are the "controller" of the details below. We are based outside the UK and have not appointed a UK data protection representative, because our processing is occasional and low-risk (UK GDPR Article 27(2)). We will review that as sales grow, and name a representative here if we appoint one.

Where your details go
Details we hold are stored in the United States, where we are based. Payhip passes your purchase details to us there under its own terms. Besides Payhip, the only others who handle them are our payment provider (PayPal or Stripe), our email provider, Kit (which runs our email list), and a professional adviser such as an accountant if we need one. We make no automated decisions about you and do no profiling.
If you buy a book
Checkout and the download are run by Payhip; under Payhip's end-user license, the download itself is supplied through Payhip Limited. Payment goes to our PayPal or Stripe account, and we never see your card details. Payhip sends us your name, email address, what you bought and when. We use that to help if a file fails, to honor our voluntary refund promise (if a file won't open, or a book isn't useful to you, email the seller address on your receipt within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you), and for our tax records. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests (support, refunds, and keeping the tax records we are required to keep). We keep those records for six years. Payhip's own privacy policy covers what Payhip holds.
If we email you at work
We sometimes write once to a named person at a limited company, at their work address, about a book relevant to their business. We use your name, work email and company, taken from where we say in that email (for example your company's website). Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in telling businesses about relevant products. After that single email we keep your name, work email and company for 24 months only so we do not email you again, then delete them. It is not a list for a second email. Reply "stop" and we delete them sooner, keeping only a short note for as long as we send marketing email, so we never contact you again.
If you join our email list
The sign-up box on our newsletter page is optional. If you use it, you give us your email address, and we use it only to send you ePORTS emails: new guides, fee and rule changes on the platforms we cover, and now and then an offer on our own books. The list is run by Kit (Kit Inc., in the United States), which stores your address and sends the emails for us under its own privacy policy. First you get one email asking you to confirm, and nothing else is sent until you click it. Like most email services, Kit records whether each email is opened and which links are clicked, so we can see which emails are useful. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can email hello@eports.global. When you unsubscribe the emails stop; Kit keeps your address marked as unsubscribed so you are not added again by mistake, and we delete it entirely if you ask. Lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
If you email us
If you write to hello@eports.global, we get your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of what we agreed (for example a refund). Our email provider stores it. We keep the conversation for up to two years after it ends, then delete it, unless it is part of a purchase record we must keep for tax. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests in answering enquiries and keeping a record of correspondence and agreed actions.
No selling or sharing
We don't sell your details or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

Your rights

If we hold details about you, you can ask us to…

  • show you what we hold
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • limit how we use it
  • give you a copy to take elsewhere
  • stop using it for our legitimate interests
  • stop contacting you, any time, no reason needed

Asking isn't an automatic yes. We won't delete purchase records we must keep for tax (six years), or the short do-not-contact note that stops us emailing you again. A copy to take elsewhere applies where the law gives you that right. If we can't do what you ask, we'll tell you why.

Email hello@eports.global. If UK data protection law applies to you and you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Links out, and changes

A last few honest notes

Some pages link to other companies — a print provider, a selling platform, a spreadsheet you open in your own software. Once you follow a link, that company's own privacy terms apply, not this one.

Effective date: 12 Nov 2026. If this notice changes in any material way, we post the new text on this page and change the effective date before the change takes effect. That posting is how we tell you. It covers any new kind of detail we collect, any new company we share with, and any longer retention period, and it always comes before analytics, advertising, a newsletter or any tracking goes live.

This is a plain-language notice, not legal advice.
