THE WEEKLY CADENCE PLANNER  —  give the business a heartbeat
From "The One-Person AI Business — Intermediate", Chapter 10

================================================================

A set of working functions is not a business that runs itself — it's a pile of
parts, and YOU are still the thing wiring them together in real time. The
missing piece isn't another function. It's the SCHEDULE that fires them. Design
your week as a handful of booked GATES; the machine runs continuously between
them. You stop being the doing with a little thinking squeezed in, and become
the thinking, with the doing handled underneath you.

⚠️ A cadence in your head is a to-do list, and a to-do list loses to whatever's
on fire. Booked in the calendar, it's a commitment the week is built around.


BOOK YOUR WEEK AROUND GATES, NOT TASKS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Gate (from ch 4–9)              Day     Time      Length
  ------------------------------  ------  --------  --------
  Approve the demand queue        ______  ________  ______
  Escalation window (daily)       daily   ________  ______
  Delivery gate (before ship)     ______  ________  ______
  Sales/support exceptions        ______  ________  ______
  Weekly review                   ______  ________  ______
  Monthly numbers ritual          ______  (once/mo) ______
  ______________________________  ______  ________  ______

  Leave deliberate SLACK for escalations and the genuinely new. Then total it:
    My scheduled founder-time at the gates:  about ______ hrs / week


SAM'S WEEK  (a reference, not a rule)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Mon 20m — approve the week's staged demand queue (risky-class posts held)
  Every weekday 15m — escalation window
  Tue 25m — delivery gate: check the brief before its Wed 7am send
  Fri 30m — weekly review
  Once a month 30m — numbers ritual
  → the GATES cost about 2.5 focused hours a week; the rest of the month's
    ~38 owner-hours is deeper judgment, not grind.


THE WEEKLY REVIEW  (30 min — how the system gets smarter)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  [ ] What did the machine do WELL this week?
  [ ] Which call did I DISAGREE with → which rule do I sharpen? (feeds ch2 brain)
  [ ] What's coming that the queue doesn't cover yet?


THE FORCING COMMITMENT  (booked and dated — book it before the week starts)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  "My operating week, booked in the calendar from ______: [Mon ___, Tue ___,
   ...], a daily escalation window at ______, the monthly numbers ritual on the
   ______. Between these blocks, the machine runs. I attend the gates and make the
   judgment calls; I no longer do the grind."
