THE LEVERAGE-CYCLE PLANNER  —  the move that raises revenue per hour, + the cadence
From "The One-Person AI Business — Advanced", Chapters 10–11
================================================================

A leverage cycle is a closed loop that raises the ratio and then FEEDS itself:
you deploy assets you already own so revenue rises while your recurring hours
don't climb above where you started, and the proceeds make the next turn easier.
This plans one cycle — and books the cadence that turns one win into years of them.


THE STRICT TEST  (what makes it leverage, not a job you gave yourself)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  Revenue per hour must RISE, and recurring hours must stay STRICTLY BELOW your
  starting plateau. One-time build hours are a separate investment (with its own payback);
  they NEVER enter the ratio's denominator.
